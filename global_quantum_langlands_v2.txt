# Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level

hypersetup{pdftitle={Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level},pdfauthor={OpenAI}}

maketitle

Abstract: 
We prove the unramified quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group G at every level cinℂ∖ℚ, including non-real levels. For every smooth projective connected complex curve X, the theorem identifies the full category of twisted D-modules on Bun_G(X) with the corresponding category on Bun_{G^vee}(X) at the dual shifted level -1/(rc), where r is the lacing number. The equivalence uses the actual global forms of both groups and preserves all connected components.

# Introduction

## Statement of the theorem

Let X be a smooth projective connected curve over ℂ and let G be a connected simple complex algebraic group. The Langlands dual G^vee is determined by the dual of the full root datum, so the global form (isogeny type) of G is part of the data. Write Bun_G(X) for the stack of principal G-bundles. The adjoint determinant line is
[
  L_G|_E=det RGamma(X,mathfrak g_E),frakg_E),
]
so det H^1 enters with exponent -1. For h^vee the dual Coxeter number and cinℂ the shifted level, set

  D_c(Bun_G(X)) := D(L_G^{(c-h^vee)/(2h^vee)}).

Here D(L^s) is the cocomplete derived DG category of all twisted D-modules for the complex power of L. The power is a sheaf of twisted differential operators; no choice of root of L is needed. Descent and sign conventions are fixed in Section~sec:foundations.

Let rin{1,2,3} be the lacing number of G. Also fix dual pinnings and a theta characteristic θ on X with θ^{ ⊗  2}~=Ω_X. These choices fix the residue characters and the localization normalizations used throughout.

[Main]
For every cinℂ∖ℚ there is an equivalence of presentable ℂ-linear DG categories

  D_c(Bun_G(X)) ~= D_{-1/(rc)}(Bun_{G^vee}(X)).

The equivalence uses the given global forms and all connected components. It is characterized by the localization--Whittaker comparison of Section~sec:equivalence, including its compatibility with families of marked points and their collisions, and it intertwines central torsor actions with the corresponding transgression local systems.


The normalization (intro:level) is the shifted-level convention of cite[Section~1.1.5, Conjecture~1]{BogdanovaBetti}. Theorem~thm:main therefore gives the unramified de Rham quantum Langlands equivalence in the irrational range. The localization comparison and the central compatibility are part of the construction: they are the two ingredients that identify the functor as the quantum Langlands correspondence. Their definitions appear with the functors they concern in Section~sec:equivalence.

## Context and prior work

Geometric Langlands relates sheaf-theoretic categories on a bundle stack to categories built from local systems for the dual group. Its quantum form compares twisted D-module categories at inverse shifted levels. The gauge-theoretic interpretation through electric--magnetic duality ([cite]) explains the appearance of the dual root datum and reciprocal parameter. An early formulation, attributed there to Drinfeld, is Stoyanovsky's inverse-parameter conjecture for twisted D-module categories cite[Section~1.3, Conjecture~2]{Stoyanovsky}. The formulation in terms of local categories, localization, and Whittaker coefficients is developed in Gaitsgory's quantum Langlands program [cite].

Several established results supply local and classical inputs. Feigin--Frenkel duality identifies principal W-algebras at inverse shifted levels [cite]. Arakawa--Frenkel identify reductions of Weyl modules and their coweight twists, including concentration and simplicity at irrational level [cite]. Raskin's categorical Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction gives a renormalized W-module category and generalized vacuum objects [cite]. The fundamental local equivalences of Campbell--Dhillon--Raskin give the required irrational-level point categories for actual global forms [cite]. Gaitsgory's local/global Whittaker comparison supplies the bridge between the affine Grassmannian and global pole models, also over multipoint parameter schemes [cite].

On the global side, the proof of ordinary geometric Langlands provides the spectral description of the tempered category and its enhanced Whittaker--Poincar'e comparison [cite]. The arbitrary-level localization and unipotent-period constructions of [cite] supply further inputs. Sections~sec:localization--sec:ran construct the period comparison with finite levels, arbitrary Grassmannian tests, and moving-point operations needed below. Drinfeld--Gaitsgory's finiteness and constant-term results and Gaitsgory's miraculous duality furnish the categorical and geometric framework for our twisted duality [cite]. Lin's comparison of Poincar'e series with miraculous duality supplies the ordinary-level geometric maps that we deform [cite].

Recent work of Bogdanova constructs a quantum Langlands functor in the Betti setting and specifies its central compatibility through 2-Fourier--Mukai duality [cite]. Her work on quantum Whittaker nonvanishing also makes explicit the finite local averaging diagram used in our argument [cite]. The two further mechanisms developed here are a weight argument controlling filtered deformation of a global kernel, and an extension argument turning local pairings into coherent comparisons across collisions.

## The two main comparisons

Localization takes representations of loop Lie algebras to twisted D-modules on the bundle stack and is a quotient functor. We first compare the two sides on localized objects, using local pairings and a global period identity. The quotient property then produces a functor between the bundle categories; two global Whittaker identities provide its left and right inverses.

The first ingredient is a global Whittaker coefficient identity. For a group H and a determinant exponent s, let R_s denote the coefficient functor with arbitrary moving sets of allowed poles. Its target remembers the nondegenerate unipotent character and the pole positions. Pairing with the opposite character and applying twisted pseudo-identity produces a Poincar'e functor J_s. We construct a comparison and prove that it is an isomorphism:

                 J_sR_s→Id
                 (snotinℚ).

The comparison is defined on the entire twisted D-module category. Its ordinary-level counterpart has an anti-tempered remainder. A fixed finite Whittaker test annihilates that remainder and is conservative at irrational exponent, so it suffices to prove that the tested kernel cone vanishes after deformation. Finite pole bounds and finite diagrams of marked-point maps give finite approximations to this cone. We express them by ordinary geometric complexes with a universal Kummer local system of monodromy q; Fourier transform in auxiliary affine lines produces the exponential characters. Quasi-unipotent monodromy makes cohomology ranks and transition-map ranks constant away from roots of unity. Compatible logarithm variations and a uniform upper weight bound imply that each cohomology generator in the completion at q=1 is killed by a finite transition. Constancy of transition ranks gives the same vanishing at every non-torsion q, hence at every irrational exponent. Ordinary geometric Langlands supplies the cohomological bound. Section~sec:deformation proves the deformation principles; Section~sec:plancherel applies them to the tested kernel cone.

The second ingredient is a local-comparison package for the two Langlands-dual groups over families of marked points. For G and G^vee together, convolution followed by Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction pairs a positive-loop-equivariant Kac--Moody module with a Whittaker object on its affine Grassmannian. At a single point, inverse-level W-algebra duality identifies reductions with the representation and modification labels exchanged. To use these pairings globally, one needs maps over the whole parameter scheme, including collision diagonals, together with insertion and factorization compatibilities. We construct the two pairings with a common W-module target and continuous relative right adjoints. On each configuration stratum, the cohomology of the semisimple group makes the adjunction units isomorphisms through degree two on heart objects. Maps between heart objects can therefore be recovered from their images in the common target. Restriction across a collision divisor has amplitude [0,1]; the recovered maps identify its boundary epimorphisms, extending the equivalence of hearts across the divisor. Realization and the adjunctions give the equivalence of DG categories. A separate extension argument, using translation of the marked points and the horizontal vacuum, identifies the pairings with both slots inserted. Sections~sec:loop--sec:local carry out these steps.

## Organization

Section~sec:foundations sets up the category-theoretic base. Irrational inertia weights eliminate sufficiently unstable Harder--Narasimhan strata. This reduces the relevant bundle categories to quasi-compact opens and allows ordinary categorical duals to be used. Section~sec:pseudoidentity proves twisted pseudo-identity using compactifications, adjunctions, and the Levi boundary of the diagonal. Section~sec:whittaker fixes the Whittaker models and their dual pairings.

After the two main comparisons, Sections~sec:localization--sec:ran identify unipotent periods with integrated reduction complexes (Theorem~loc:period-comparison). The proof keeps the determinant lines, finite-level shifts, and moving-point operators throughout. Ran integration contracts the added point variables and turns the local pairing comparison into a global period identity.

Section~sec:equivalence assembles the equivalence through the localization quotient, using the period identity and the two instances of (intro:plancherel). It also derives the exchange of components and central characters, and the coherent action of central torsors.

# Twists, boundedness, and categorical duality

The kernel calculations below take place in categories of all twisted D-modules on bundle stacks. Two foundations are needed: a precise twist choice, and a finiteness statement saying that at the irrational exponents in question restriction to a suitable quasi-compact open is an equivalence. We establish these, then the kernel formalism and its renormalized evaluation pairing.

## Root data, determinant lines, level conventions

All groups retain their given root data. Fix dual pinnings and a theta characteristic θ with θ^{ ⊗  2}~=Ω_X. For a connected reductive group H write
[
 Y_H=Bun_H(X),
 L_H|_E=det RGamma(X,mathfrak h_E).frakh_E).
]
Thus det H^1 enters with exponent -1. Write rho_H for the half-sum of positive coroots, and set P_0=θ^{2rho_H}, an H-bundle via the integral cocharacter 2rho_H. In Grassmannian constructions rigidify L_H to L_H ⊗ (L_H|_{P_0})^{-1}; a constant line gives the same twisted differential operators.

For a line L on a smooth stack Y and sinℂ, write D(L^s) for the presentable DG category of twisted D-modules. Sign convention: if a frame e is replaced by ae, the local differentiation changes by -s,dlog a. Equivalently, on the frame torsor of L we impose strong Kummer equivariance with the connection

 d-s,dlog t, q=exp(2pi i s).

The associated de Rham local system has monodromy q. On stacks these categories and functors use strong derived descent. The opposite twist is L^{-s}; sums of twists use the tensor product of frame torsors.

We use sheaf conventions: omega_Y unit of  ⊗ ^!, k_Y constant object, lower star for de Rham direct image. For left D-modules on smooth Y write M^{< Y>} for the left module M[dim Y]; then O_Y^{< Y>}=omega_Y. Inside angle brackets !-pullback is left crystalline pull without extra shift; the convention descends to smooth stacks with their stack dimension. The exponential object of phase phi is the left connection d+dphi in dualizing normalization. All tensor products and mapping complexes are derived; categories are cocomplete, ℂ-linear, and functors are continuous unless an adjoint or partial functor is specified.

Use the basic invariant forms Kil/(2h^vee) and Kil^vee/(2check h^vee). Inverse forms give long roots, resp. long coroots, squared length 2. Inverse shifted forms have parameters a,d with

 a=-c, d=frac1{ra}=-frac1{rc},
 k_a=a-h^vee, k_d=d-check h^vee.

Our four categories are

 A=D(L_G^{-k_a/(2h^vee)}), A'=D(L_G^{k_a/(2h^vee)}),
 B'=D(L_{G^vee}^{-k_d/(2check h^vee)}), B=D(L_{G^vee}^{k_d/(2check h^vee)}).

In the convention of cite[Section~1.1.5]{BogdanovaBetti}, a shifted level c means exponent (c-h^vee)/(2h^vee). Since
[
 -(k_a)/(2h^vee)=(c-h^vee)/(2h^vee)+1,
]
tensoring by L_G identifies the source category of Theorem~thm:main with A. The target at shifted level -1/(rc) is exactly B. This uses an integral translation by the determinant line; it requires neither a square root of that line nor divisibility of the basic form in the lattice of integral levels. Since cnotinℚ all four exponents are irrational.

We retain all connected components:

 pi_0(Y_H)=pi_1(H)=X_*(T_H)/ℤPhi_H^vee.

Modification labels and dual weights always belong to the lattices of the specified groups. For semisimple H, the finite centre acts trivially on L_H; central inertia therefore gives the usual decomposition into character sectors, and these sectors are retained below.

## Bounded support in the categorical sense

Let H be connected reductive and let V be a finite-dimensional self-dual representation. Put
[
 L(V)|_E=det RGamma(X,V_E), Q(z,w)=Tr_V(z w),
]
and assume Q is positive definite on the real cocharacter space. For a degree uinpi_1(H) its rational image has a representative u_{cen}in X_*(Z(H)^0) ⊗ ℚ; write Q(z,u) for Q(z,u_{cen}) when z is central.

[Inertia vanishing]
Let Z be an algebraic stack carrying a line L. Suppose a central 𝔾_m in its inertia acts on L with fixed nonzero integral weight m. If smnotinℤ, then D_Z(L^s)=0. The assertion remains valid after restricting to a locally closed substack and after taking a product with a spectator stack carrying any external twist.


Proof. 
Pull to the frame torsor of L. The inertial 𝔾_m-scaling of frames is 2-isomorphic to the projection, so strong Kummer equivariance identifies the two pullbacks of an object to 𝔾_mtimes Z up to the factor t|-> t^{sm}. De Rham direct image along 𝔾_m kills the nonintegral Kummer factor (d has eigenvalues n-sm on t^n, all invertible); the constant factor survives as H^bullet_{dR}(𝔾_m), which contains ℂ as a summand. Tensoring with that complex detects zero. Smooth pull and frame-torsor pull are conservative, so the original object vanishes. The calculation leaves all spectator factors unchanged.


[Bounded support]
For snotinℚ, the category D_{Y_H}(L(V)^s) vanishes on every component of nontorsion degree. There is a quasi-compact open j:U↪ Y_H contained in the union of the torsion-degree components, such that

 j^!:D_{Y_H}(L(V)^s)xrightarrow{ sim }D_U(L(V)^s)

with inverse j_*. The same open works for -s, and the equivalence persists after taking a product with any spectator stack and any external twist there.


Proof. 
Suppose u has nonzero rational image. Nondegeneracy on the centre gives a central cocharacter z with Q(z,u)ne0. Decompose V into central weight spaces. Riemann--Roch gives the z-weight of L(V) on degree u as

 sum_mumu(z)chi(X,V_{mu,E})=Q(z,u),

the 1-g term vanishing because V is self-dual. The right side is a nonzero integer, so Lemma~foundations:inertia gives vanishing on that component, and after restriction to any locally closed bundle condition inside it.

Now fix a torsion degree. Use the characteristic-zero Harder--Narasimhan theorem for principal bundles: canonical parabolic reductions, discrete set of HN types with bounded denominators, bounded regions with quasi-compact opens cite[Sections~7.3--7.4 and~10.1]{DrinfeldGaitsgoryBun}. Let nu be the dominant rational HN coweight, with zero central component. Put b_X=max{0,2g(X)-2}. We claim the twisted category on the HN stratum vanishes when alpha_i(nu)>b_X for some simple root alpha_i.

Let Q_i be the maximal standard parabolic omitting alpha_i, M_i its Levi. The canonical reduction sits inside Q_i; projecting to M_i identifies the HN stratum as a stack of unipotent extensions over the corresponding stratum for M_i. Filter rad(Q_i) by alpha_i-height. Each quotient has semistable associated graded pieces whose slopes are beta(nu) for roots beta containing alpha_i with positive coefficient; dominance gives beta(nu)geqalpha_i(nu)>b_X, so Serre duality and semistability give H^1=0 for every such piece, hence for each successive extension. Riemann--Roch then makes the H^0's have constant rank. The extension stacks are, locally in families, successive classifying stacks of smooth unipotent groups. Choose a positive integral multiple z of the ith fundamental coweight, central in M_i. On the split section, the determinant filtration identifies L(V) with the restricted M_i-determinant line, whose z-weight is Q(z,nu)>0. Lemma~foundations:inertia kills the pullback to the split section, and smooth descent kills the whole HN stratum.

For the remaining HN types, 0leqalpha_i(nu)<= b_X for every i, so they lie in a bounded region with finitely many types; there are finitely many torsion degrees in pi_1(H). Choose a quasi-compact HN open U containing all these types. On the complement, restriction to every HN stratum vanishes; on each bounded open in the HN exhaustion, the localization triangles prove the twisted category of the complement vanishes. The exhaustion detects objects, so the localization triangle for j gives (foundations:cutoff-equivalence), with inverse j_*. The argument is unchanged for -s.


The assertion is about !-restriction of categories; it makes no set-theoretic claim about closures of supports. For simple G one takes V=mathfrak g;frakg; for a Levi, the restriction of the adjoint supplies a self-dual positive trace form.

## Operations with compatible twists

A QCA stack is quasi-compact with affine automorphism groups. We use the D-module formalism of [cite]: compact generation, categorical duality, renormalized de Rham direct image with base change and projection formula. A quasi-compact morphism is safe when the neutral components of the automorphism groups in its geometric fibres are unipotent. Safe direct image equals ordinary de Rham *-image and is continuous; schematic quasi-compact morphisms are examples cite[Sections~9--10]{DrinfeldGaitsgoryFiniteness}.

For correspondences with compatible twists the same formulas apply: compatibility identifies the pullbacks of frame torsors, or trivializes their ratio. Pulling to the torsors reduces !-base change, the !-tensor projection formula, and continuity to the ordinary formulas. The identifications commute with frame scaling, so descend with the prescribed Kummer equivariance.

We use the six operations on locally holonomic objects wherever they are defined. For schematic separated finite-type maps, shriek direct image on holonomic arguments is the partial left adjoint to !-pullback, tested against arbitrary D-modules. Verdier duality negates the twist; both can be checked on smooth charts. Riemann--Hilbert is used only for regular holonomic objects, again on charts; Kummer connections are regular singular. For a smooth map q of relative dimension e: q^*=q^![-2e].

## Compact generation and opposite-twist duality


Let Y be a smooth quasi-compact open in a bundle stack, tau a determinant-line twist. Then D_tau(Y) is compactly generated; on every smooth finite-type scheme chart the pullback of a compact object has bounded coherent cohomology. External tensor product induces an equivalence

 D_{tau_1}(Y_1) ⊗  D_{tau_2}(Y_2)xrightarrow{ sim }D_{tau_1boxplustau_2}(Y_1times Y_2).

The categorical dual of D_tau(Y) is D_{-tau}(Y) with evaluation and coevaluation

 (M,N)⟼Gamma_{dR}^{ren}(Y,M ⊗ ^!N), Delta_*omega_Y.

The twist on the diagonal is canonically trivial. These assertions persist after the cutoff of Proposition~foundations:cutoff.


Proof. 
Oblivion and induction. Write tau=s,dlog L, with frame torsor P=L^timesto Y. An object N of D_tau(Y) is represented on P by a strongly Kummer-equivariant D-module. Forgetting the connection in normalized left-module convention turns the Kummer connection d-s,dlog t into the trivial line with frame 1, and smooth !-pullback then induces ordinary pullback of the underlying quasi-coherent complex, with no dimension shift. The equivariance isomorphisms give quasi-coherent descent along Pto Y, retaining all isotropy actions. On a chart trivializing L the operation is right-module oblivion tensored with (omega_Y^{can})^{-1}[-d] (dim Y=d).

We shall construct Ind_tau on Perf(Y) with

 RHom_{D_tau(Y)}(Ind_tau(F),N)~= RGamma(Y,F^vee ⊗  N^{oblv}).

Since perfect complexes compactly generate QCoh(Y), this gives compact generators; external-product compatibility gives Künneth.

Presentation and induction. The bounded Quot construction presents Y as a quotient of a smooth finite-type scheme by a product of general linear groups. Choosing a uniform twist, faithfulness bounds the associated vector bundles, and framing their sections gives a scheme atlas; reductions of structure group are schemes of sections because the quotient by the reductive subgroup is affine. Choose a smooth presentation p:Zto Y torsor for such a product.

The twisted induction is the relative Lie-algebroid construction of cite[Section~6.3, Lemmas~6.3.15--6.3.16]{DrinfeldGaitsgoryFiniteness}. For a smooth chart Tto Y let A_T=T_{T/Y} be the relative tangent Lie algebroid, with anchor to T_T. Relative differentiation lifts A_T to the Atiyah algebroid of the pulled-back line, hence to its TDO D_T^: for ain A_T with anchor v_a acting on a local frame by b_a, the operator is v_a+s,b_a. Under e'=fe both b_a changes by v_a(log f) and the differentiation generator by -s,v_a(log f), so the operator is frame-independent. Define for FinPerf(Y)

 Ind_tau(F)|_T=(D_T^{ ⊗ }^{L}_{U(A_T)}F_T)^{< T>}.

F_T carries its canonical relative connection. Kernels in the anchor are retained: their action on the twisting line gives the corresponding isotropy scalar in D_T^ Trivializing the line does not discard this isotropy action.

For a smooth map T'to T over Y, the algebroid A_{T'} is the inverse image algebroid; resolving first in T_{T'/T} by the Spencer complex gives precisely the transfer bimodule for smooth pullback applied to (foundations:induction). Two smooth maps compose, so the chart objects descend. At zero twist, (foundations:induction) equals cite[Section~6.3.21]{DrinfeldGaitsgoryFiniteness} applied, in IndCoh normalization, to F ⊗ omega_Y^{can}[d]; on BH with omega_{BH}^{can}=detmathfrak h,frakh, the factors cancel the det(mathfrak h^frakh^vee)[dim H] of cite[Section~6.4.8]{DrinfeldGaitsgoryFiniteness}, and one recovers derived mathfrak h-coinvariantsfrakh-coinvariants on the point atlas.

# Twisted pseudo-identity

The ordinary and extraordinary diagonal kernels play different roles. The former represents categorical duality (Section~sec:foundations); the latter must itself be shown to be an equivalence. We prove this by combining parabolic adjunctions with a compactification of the diagonal. The essential additional point at irrational twist is that central inertia removes every boundary stratum with nonzero defect.

Let H be connected reductive, V a self-dual representation with positive-definite trace form Q on the real cocharacter space, and put L_H=det RGamma(X,V_E). Restrictions of V define the corresponding lines for Levi subgroups. Write
[
 C_H=D(L_H^s), C'_H=D(L_H^calC'_H=D(L_H^{-s}), snotinℚ.
]
Section~sec:foundations identifies (C'_H)^calC'_H)^vee with C_H; the line ratio on the diagonal is canonically trivial. The kernel Delta_!k_{Y_H} defines a continuous endofunctor F_H of C_H; its existence as a kernel uses that the diagonal is schematic and that k_{Y_H} is locally holonomic.


For every H,V,s as above, the functor F_H is an equivalence. The assertion holds on all components (including those on which the twisted category is zero) and also with s replaced by -s.


The proof is by induction on semisimple rank. We first establish parabolic adjunctions compatible with F_H. A compactification of the diagonal will then show that the cone of F_HtoId[-2dim Y_H] has a finite filtration whose quotients factor through proper Levi categories. On Eisenstein-induced objects F_H is computed by (eq:M4); on objects with vanishing proper constant terms F_H acts by a shift. These generation inputs establish the equivalence.

The geometric inputs are geometric Eisenstein series and second adjointness [cite]. Twists and coherence require attention; an adjunction valid only on holonomic objects would not suffice, so we work on all twisted D-modules.

## Parabolic correspondences and their compactifications

For the moment let H be semisimple. Fix a parabolic P, its Levi M, an opposite P^-, and a torsion degree uinpi_1(M). Set Y=Y_H and use the correspondence
[
 Y_M^uxleftarrow{ q }Y_P^uxrightarrow{ p }Y.
]
The weight filtration of V attached to P gives p^*L_H~= q^*L_M; normalize it to be the identity on split M-reductions. Use the same normalization for P^-. Define
[
 C_P=q_*p^!, E_P=p_*q^!, e=dim(q)=(g(X)-1)dim N_P,
]
the last equality using that u is torsion (the value is the same for P^-). Primes on these functors mean the opposite twist.

Here p is schematic, separated, relatively quasi-compact; q is smooth, relatively quasi-compact, safe. A positive grading of N_P presents q, locally over the Levi stack, as successive fibrations of vector stacks, each of the form [V^1/V^0] for a two-term complex computing cohomology on X; there are no obstructions in degree two. Three consequences: C_P,E_P are continuous; q_* preserves local regular holonomicity; and the partial holonomic operations q_!,q^* have their true adjunction against arbitrary D-modules. For the last, present each vector stack by its vector bundle: descent along the vector group is fully faithful by de Rham acyclicity, and both assertions reduce to schematic formulas. For a:V^1to[V^1/V^0], a_*a^*~=Id and a_!a^!~=Id follow from ordinary and compactly supported cohomology of vector-group fibers; the shifts survive when the vector stack has negative dimension. These reductions commute with smooth descent and with adding a spectator.

Kernel duality gives
[
 (C'_P)^vee=E_P.
]
Both kernels are obtained from the diagonal unit by !-base change followed by safe direct image. Every calculation may be made on quasi-compact open exhaustions; Proposition~foundations:cutoff identifies the twisted categories of the bundle stacks with those on fixed quasi-compact opens. No co-category is introduced.

Use the Drinfeld compactification
[
 Y_P^uxrightarrow{ j }widetilde Y_P^u,
 barbar q:q:widetilde Y_P^u→ Y_M^u,
 barbar p:p:widetilde Y_P^u→ Y.
]
Its points are generalized reductions with the Levi datum retained, barbar pp is schematic and proper. If the derived group is simply connected, this is compactification by generically nondegenerate sections of the affine closure of H/N_P; the boundary stratifies by positive defects, and on each stratum the saturated reduction is an actual P-bundle whose Levi bundle is related to the specified one by a positive modification. The map from the stratum to this Levi Hecke datum is the base change of the ordinary map from parabolic bundles to Levi bundles; the stratification is locally finite on quasi-compact substacks [cite].

For actual global form, choose a central extension H_1to H with torus kernel D and simply-connected derived group, form the compactification upstairs, and quotient by the D-action.
