

A gas of billiard balls. A large box holds identical hard spheres,
like a three-dimensional break of pool: they fly in straight lines and
bounce elastically on contact. The diameter ε is tiny and the density
is mu=ε^{-2} spheres per unit volume, so the gas is dilute yet the
number of spheres is enormous.

The average. The expected number of spheres per unit volume of
position-velocity space, the one-particle density, solves the Boltzmann
equation as ε↓0. That is Lanford's kinetic limit.

The wobble. The random gas equals this deterministic density plus
a small error. Scaling the error by sqrtmu gives the fluctuation field
zeta^ε_t; the question is its law as ε↓0.

The answer. The scaled error is asymptotically Gaussian, with a
covariance governed by the fluctuating Boltzmann equation: the linearized
Boltzmann drift plus a Gaussian noise whose strength is the collision rate,

d zeta_t(phi)=zeta_t(A_tphi) d t+ d W_t(phi),

where A_t is the linearized generator and W is a noise of covariance
C_t, both fixed below. This is a finite-dimensional, multi-time central
limit theorem for a deterministic gas.

Not obvious. The spheres interact deterministically; there is no
random force and no resampling. The Gaussian limit is emergent, produced by
the random initial configuration together with the extreme sensitivity of
which pairs collide. Both effects must be tracked, not averaged over.

What was known. The derivation descends from the BBGKY hierarchy of
Bogolyubov and Kirkwood, from the Boltzmann--Grad limit for rarefied gases,
and from the Hilbert and Chapman--Enskog expansions that organize the
hydrodynamic corrections. Lanford proved short-time validity of the
Boltzmann equation. Uchiyama gave a different derivation, and
Gallagher--Saint-Raymond--Texier later recast the proof so that the
geometric and probabilistic content separate cleanly. Van Beijeren--Lanford--Lebowitz--Spohn and Spohn
computed the fluctuation covariance. Pulvirenti--Simonella and
Bodineau--Gallagher--Saint-Raymond--Simonella built the quantitative
short-time fluctuation theory, the latter with process-level convergence,
the Gaussian collision noise, and exact microscopic centering, on a periodic
box; at equilibrium the same group reached the long-time Gaussian limit.
Deng--Hani--Ma carried the averaged kinetic limit to the full regular
Boltzmann lifespan on all of space. The new step is to carry the
fluctuation theorem to that same lifespan, on all of space, away from
equilibrium.

Two new difficulties. First, size: the h-th factorial cumulant,
the connected h-point correlation, must be pinned at the sharp scale
mu^{1-h}, far below a crude bound. A change of relative translations of
collision clusters extracts one ε^2 per merging collision and does it.
Second, centering: the gas is centered by its true microscopic mean, which
differs from the Boltzmann mean already at order ε, since hard spheres
cannot overlap; at the scale sqrtmu=ε^{-1} that difference survives,
so the model dynamics must match the gas with probability better than
O(ε).

Why one collision costs ε^2. Two groups of spheres that
evolved independently can join one connected history only if, at the
contact, their relative position lands exactly on a sphere of radius ε
around the other group. Their relative motion is a straight line, and a line
meets that tiny sphere in a window whose cross section is a disk of area of
order ε^2, hit during a sliding time interval. This is the source of the
factor ε^2 per merging collision, the merger Jacobian
ε^2[(v-v_*)·omega] d t d omega. The gas carries activity
mu=ε^{-2} per particle, so a connected diagram with h marked
particles and m lines weighs mu^{m-h}, and each merger pays one ε^2:
the two powers of ε cancel line by line. The initial excluded-volume link
is a ball, not a sphere, so it costs ε^3, the only place where the
static problem is even smaller than the dynamical one.

Where does the Gaussian randomness come from? A test sees a sum of very many
almost-independent contributions, one from each close encounter. Whether a
given near-encounter happens is decided by microscopic sensitivity, so each
contribution acts like an independent bounded random variable. A sum of many
such variables is Gaussian by the central limit theorem, and its variance is
the collision rate, exactly C_t. The body of the argument proves the gas is
close enough to this picture for it to hold throughout the regular lifespan
and on the whole space.

Remark (The initial covariance). The initial covariance has no subtraction of
(int f_0phi)(int f_0psi): the particle number is random. More
importantly, microscopic centering is not the same as centering by the
Boltzmann mean. With ρ_0(x)=int f_0(x,v) d v,

lim_{εto0}sqrtmu(Epi_0^ε(phi)-int f_0phi)
=-(4pi)/(3)int f_0(x,v)phi(x,v)ρ_0(x) d x d v .

So exclusion shifts the mean by order ε, which survives the factor
sqrtmu=ε^{-1}. Centering by the Boltzmann density alone would
leave a nonzero deterministic drift already at time zero. This is why
the proof must compare exact expectations, not merely laws.

The layered collision-history framework of Deng--Hani--Ma is kept, stated as the
black box the analytic package. Its role is to remove large
complexity: histories with many entangled collisions. What remains must
be controlled at the fluctuation scale, and that is where the new work
lies.

1. Initial ensemble and Gaussian target
(the initial ensemble, below). Exact initial cumulants, the initial CLT,
the shift, the initial-covariance remark, and construction of the Gaussian
field of the fluctuation limit theorem.

2. Connected expansion with records
(the connected expansion, below). Factorial cumulants are written as a
finite sum of globally connected diagrams, on a single pasted dynamics,
keeping finitely many observation records. Large complexity is dropped
by the Deng--Hani--Ma package.

3. Sharp correlation bounds (the sharp bounds, below,
the sharp connected bounds). The h-th cumulant is O(mu^{1-h}),
with the leading h=1,2 terms identified as signed sums of trees
weighted by f_{itau}, contact flux, and pm1 signs. This is proved
by chronological merger coordinates: each collision that merges two
clusters contributes ε^2[(v-v_*)·omega]_+, and summing all
clusters costs CT/sqrt L<1.

4. Gaussian limit for the model dynamics
(the Gaussian limit of the model dynamics, below). A mark that accumulates observations turns
every fixed multi-time linear combination into one scalar whose
ordinary cumulants are computed; second cumulant converges, higher ones
vanish, giving the Gaussian law with the correct covariance.

5. Accuracy of the model dynamics (the cutoff comparison, below,
the pasted accuracy bound). The pasted and true dynamics agree with
probability O(ε^{1+eta}), eta=1/20. This is a positive
geometric estimate on terminal witnesses, carried out with local
gains, a common birth-energy bound, and a causal time-order sum free of
per-vertex logarithms.

6. Exact centering transfer (the centering transfer, below). The
discrepancy estimate, the fixed moments of N/mu, and H"older's
ineuality show that expectations agree to o(mu^{-1/2}), which is
what transfers the limit with exact centering.

This stage is independent of the dynamics. It does two things: prove the
fluctuation limit at time zero (a pure equilibrium statement about the
grand-canonical hard-sphere measure), and construct the Gaussian field
that the fluctuation limit theorem claims to be the limit. It also records the initial
correlation bounds that later parts use.

Remark (Order of choices). The local exponent delta and the finite hierarchy of
the amplified cut fix Gamma_0 from dimensional constants. Then
choose Gamma, then L for the imported estimates and the common
exponential base; only afterward choose K, the localization powers, and the
small upper bound on ε. No observation number or moment order enters the
first two choices. This is what allows a single pasted dynamics to be fixed in
the Gaussian limit of the model dynamics, below.

The pasted process of the pasted Gaussian limit is centered by its own
microscopic mean. The real gas is centered by a slightly different mean,
since the pasted and true laws charge the discrepancy event (probability
le Cε^{1+eta}, the pasted accuracy bound) with different mass. At the
fluctuation scale sqrtmu=ε^{-1} this difference of means must go to
zero, and that is exactly the sharp estimate of the pasted accuracy bound. This is made
quantitative, then the fluctuation limit theorem is assembled.

Remark (What is exact and what is asymptotic). The subtracted mean is the exact microscopic
