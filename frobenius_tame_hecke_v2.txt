# Frobenius Structures on Tame Hecke Eigensheaves



maketitle

Abstract: 
Let nge 2 and let F_q have characteristic p>n. Let X_0 be a smooth
projective geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over F_q,
with a rational point x_0, and let rhocolonpi_1(U_0)toPGL_n(L) be a
continuous arithmetic representation with dense geometric image and tame
regular-unipotent inertia at x_0, where U_0=X_0∖{x_0} and
L/Q_ell is finite. We construct, after extending scalars to a finite field
F_{q^m}, a nonzero locally constructible perverse Weil sheaf M_0 on the
stack of G-bundles on X_0 with B-reduction at x_0, G=SL_n. Its
entire multi-leg Hecke eigenstructure is defined over F_{q^m} and has
eigenvalue the restriction of rho; its geometric singular support lies in
the parabolic nilpotent cone. The proof first realizes the geometric
eigenobject as the image of a spectral skyscraper, then makes it invariant
under Frobenius, and finally specializes back to the finite field.

# Introduction

A Hecke eigensheaf turns a local system on a curve into a sheaf on the
moduli stack of bundles, compatible with all Hecke correspondences. Over a
finite field, a local system rho carries information its geometric
restriction forgets: the action of Frobenius. An automorphic realization of
the whole arithmetic datum must respect this extra structure.

## The result

Fix G=SL_n with upper-triangular Borel Bsubset G and dual group
Gd=PGL_n. For a pointed smooth projective curve (X,x), let
Bun_{G,B,x}(X) parametrize G-bundles on X together with a B-reduction
at x. A complex on this stack is locally constructible perverse when
its pullback to each finite-type smooth chart, shifted by the relative
dimension of that chart, is bounded constructible and perverse; bounds may
depend on the chart. The parabolic global nilpotent cone
Λ_{} consists of generically nilpotent Higgs fields with
at most a simple pole at x and residue in the nilradical of the chosen
Borel. Singular support is defined chartwise on those smooth charts.

For a finite set I and representations V_i of Gd, iin I, write
Hecke_{I,boldsymbol V} for the multi-leg Hecke functor with legs in
(X∖{x})^I. We use the relative Satake normalization of
Section~sec:conventions: its tensor unit is exterior product with the
constant E-sheaf, and it carries no moving-leg shift.

A Weil structure over F_{q^m} on a geometric complex M is an
isomorphism between the q^m-Frobenius pullback of M and M; the
eigenisomorphisms of M must respect this structure and the Frobenius
structure on the parameter.


Let nge 2, let F_q have characteristic p>n, let ellne p, and put

## The two obstructions

The geometric construction of [cite] already yields a locally constructible
perverse eigenobject with fusion-compatible eigenstructure and parabolic
nilpotent singular support. Two new problems must be solved to make it
arithmetic:

[(O1)]
 An invariant parameter does not by itself make a geometrically chosen
  eigenobject visibly invariant. Some choice of eigenobject must be recognized
  from internal structure rather than from an uncontrolled equivalence.
 At a separating node used for degeneration, the boundary parameters of
  the two components face opposite orientations. The identifications that glue
  them must commute with Frobenius and must be realized by a compact
  conjugator, so that the resulting parameter is continuous with compact
  image.


Sections sec:conventions--sec:final address these in order.

## Strategy of the proof

For a dense unramified parameter in characteristic zero, the relevant
component of the restricted spectral stack is a smooth formal space supported
at one geometric point. Semilinear transport preserves it. The inverse image
of the skyscraper on that component has scalar endomorphisms and no negative
self-extensions; these two properties identify its transport up to shift
(Lemma~lem:point-object). An invariant polarization removes the shift on
a bounded open, giving an isomorphism with the transport. Density of the
parameter and triviality of Z(PGL_n) then force compatibility with the full
eigenstructure. This is Section~sec:unramified.

The arithmetic lift is built in Section~sec:parameters. A smooth
projective pointed lift of (X_0,x_0) over W(F_q) carries a tower of finite
torsors obtained by extending rho's finite quotients across root stacks; the
tower recovers rho together with all of its arithmetic structure. At the
puncture the Frobenius matrix A and the regular nilpotent logarithm N_1
satisfy Ad(A)N_1=qN_1. A cocharacter commuting with A scales N_1; a
suitable power makes the conjugator lie in a fixed compact open, though it
commutes with A. A ramified cover of controlled degree multiplies boundary
monodromy by an integer and thereby produces an auxiliary component whose
boundary acts by the inverse of the first. Gluing on a balanced twisted nodal
model then lifts to a dense unramified parameter on the smooth generic fiber.

Section~sec:nodal applies the unramified result to this glued parameter,
specializes on the prescribed node type, restricts to the first component, and
removes the enhancement of the flag. Nonvanishing and equivariance are checked
separately; perverse cohomology supplies nilpotent singular support, and the
unipotence of the boundary monodromy controls the torus direct image.

Section~sec:final specializes back to the finite field. A second
relative family over R=W(k) lands on the original pointed curve, and the
field-level nilpotent-detection theorem of cite[Theorem~4.1]{CT} produces the
parabolic cone from the eigenstructure.

## Relation to earlier work

Drinfeld's rank-two work and Laumon's geometric formulation established central
parts of geometric Langlands [cite]. Frenkel--Gaitsgory--Vilonen
constructed unramified GL_n eigensheaves, including the finite-field Weil
setting [cite]. For general groups, the restricted
spectral stack and its action on automorphic sheaves with nilpotent singular
support were developed in [cite]; the positive-characteristic
specialization is due to Gaitsgory--Raskin [cite]. The geometric input of the
present paper is the companion note [cite], which constructs locally
constructible perverse eigensheaves with Borel level, parabolic nilpotent
singular support, and fusion-compatible eigenstructure. That note does not
supply Frobenius descent; the present paper provides it, for SL_n, under
the hypotheses of Theorem~thm:main.

In characteristic-zero de Rham sheaf theory, Fae rgeman constructs coherent
tame eigenobjects for irreducible regular-singular parameters [cite],
conditional on stated inputs. We work in the geometric ell-adic setting and
establish perversity and the finite-field Weil action outright, for the dense
parameters with regular-unipotent boundary monodromy above.

The nodal framework is part of the automorphic gluing of Nadler--Yun
[cite]; twisted curves and root stacks are used as in
[cite]. Sheaf-theoretic
specialization is geometric nearby cycles
[cite].

# Equivariant Hecke systems and specialization

We fix sheaf conventions and identify which parts of the geometric
specialization formalism carry a semilinear action. The compatibility of the
whole Hecke system, including collision diagonals of moving legs, is the
substance.

## Satake transport and its Weil normalization


Semilinear isomorphisms of pointed curves identify the relative Satake and
Hecke systems, compatibly with all the operations in
Definition~def:eigenstructure. In representation labels the required
symmetric tensor comparison is unique. The same assertion holds for extension
between algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields.


Proof. 
Transport preserves the split Schubert orbits, their intersection complexes,
convolution diagrams, and fusion diagrams. It thus gives a symmetric tensor
equivalence of the spherical Satake categories preserving all highest-weight
labels. Under Satake this is a symmetric tensor autoequivalence of
Rep_E(PGL_n) with trivial outer automorphism. Over the algebraically closed
coefficient field it is tensor-isomorphic to the identity: the fiber-functor
torsor is trivial and the remaining group automorphism is inner. Two such
isomorphisms differ by a tensor automorphism of the identity functor, i.e. an
element of Z(PGL_n), which is trivial.

On separated legs this is the exterior-product comparison. Fusion
extensions, proper convolution maps, and their exchange transformations are
transported together, so their compatibility maps agree. In the kernel
categories the fusion extensions are middle extensions with the usual perverse
leg shifts; a comparison on the separated locus determines them. Applying the
corresponding natural sheaf operations gives the Hecke comparison. Algebraically
closed base extension has the same properties: it preserves the simple kernels
and convolution and is an equivalence on spherical Satake. Uniqueness again
identifies its representation labels.



Over a finite field, the split Weil structures on
(eq:satake-normalization) give a symmetric tensor Satake system, with
ordinary coefficient multiplicity spaces carrying trivial action. Its
Frobenius comparison is the one of Lemma~lem:satake-transport.


Proof. 
Purity alone does not specify the Frobenius scalars, so we verify the
normalization. Resolve a split affine Schubert variety by a Bott--Samelson
resolution using the Iwahori stratification; its iterated projective-bundle
structure is defined over the finite field. The projective-bundle formula gives
scalar geometric Frobenius q^j on H^{2j} and zero odd cohomology. After the
shift and twist [d_lambda](d_lambda/2), the scalar on degree i is
q^{i/2}.

In perverse degree zero of the proper direct image, the full-support
intersection complex occurs with multiplicity one. The decomposition theorem
cite[Theorems~5.3.8 and~5.4.5]{BBD} makes its isotypic summand geometrically
well defined and hence Frobenius stable, with the normalization fixed on the
open orbit. The Frobenius-stable perverse Leray filtration, whose spectral
sequence degenerates by the same theorem, exhibits each cohomology group of
this summand as a subquotient of the corresponding scalar cohomology group.
Thus
[
 H^i(Gr,IC_lambda) has Frobenius as on E(-i/2)
 (i even), H^i=0quad(i odd).
]

The cohomological convolution comparison is graded and Frobenius compatible:
it is constructed by K"unneth on separated legs and fusion over the split
affine line cite[Lemma~6.1, Equations~(6.2a)--(6.2c), and Section~14]{MV}.
The cohomology system is lisse across collisions and geometrically constant; it
is constant on the separated locus in coordinate trivializations, and
lisseness extends this constancy. Comparison between rational configurations
therefore commutes with Frobenius; binary tensor comparison uses (0,1) and
(0,0), available over every finite field. The scalar rule is consequently
the same for convolution and for the corresponding tensor product. Every
graded tensor comparison on total cohomology commutes with these scalars.
Faithfulness of the cohomological Satake fiber functor shows that the kernel
tensor maps do as well. In particular, the induced Frobenius action on the
geometric multiplicity space Hom(IC_nu,IC_lambda*IC_mu) is trivial:
source and target cohomology have the same scalar in each degree, and the
cohomological fiber functor is faithful. Fusion extends the compatibility to
the relative system, and uniqueness in Lemma~lem:satake-transport
identifies this Weil comparison with the transport comparison.


## The specialization input with an action

The geometric result we need is stronger than one-point Hecke commutation. It
is the combination of Propositions~2.1 and~5.1 of [cite] with the following

the closed point. Let mathcal B/ScalB/S be a smooth locally finite-type bundle

Bounded Hecke correspondences have proper representable output maps, and in
coordinates and input frames they have the split Schubert local models with
their spherical kernels. The setup includes ordinary and enhanced level
disjoint from the legs, and the twisted nodal families used below.

[Geometric specialization and transport]

In this setup, geometric nearby cycles preserve local bounded constructibility
and are perverse exact. They admit natural isomorphisms

 Hecke^s_{I,boldsymbol V}(Psi F)
  xrightarrow{ sim }
  Psi(Hecke^{bareta}_{I,boldsymbol V}(F))

compatible with the unit, convolution, permutations, and every fusion
diagonal. Suppose F has a coherent eigenstructure with parameter
θ_{bareta}. For each representation, suppose its evaluation sheaf
has a lisse lattice whose finite reductions extend lisse after finite trait
extensions, compatibly on further extensions, and with the reductions of a

If the models, F with its given coherent eigenstructure, and these

invariant trait extensions. No single finite extension is required for all

Proof. 
The geometric assertions are exactly cite[Propositions~2.1 and~5.1]{CT}; the
nearby-cycle formalism is described in cite[Section~4.1]{GR} and
cite[Theorems~4.1 and~6.7]{HansenScholze}. These are geometric nearby cycles
without inertia invariants, and the input need not descend over one finite
extension of the fraction field.

Equivariance is additional. An isomorphism of traits and models, together with
its lift to the geometric generic point, identifies the defining nearby-cycle
diagrams. The pullback, proper-pushforward, and tensor exchange maps therefore
commute with its transport. In input frames the Hecke comparison is the natural
exterior-product exchange with a Satake kernel, followed by proper-pushforward
exchange; this is the construction of cite[Section~5]{CT}, including its
comparison on collision diagonals. The Satake-label identification of
cite[Lemma~5.3]{CT} also commutes with transport: the two composites are
tensor comparisons and hence agree by Lemma~lem:satake-transport.

On the eigenvalue side, the lisse tensor comparison is natural at every finite
lattice reduction and every further trait extension; passing to the adic
system preserves this naturality. Thus (eq:hecke-specialization)
transports the equivariant eigenmaps and all their diagrams. The convolution
and nested diagonal identities are identities of the same exchange
transformations and require no separate choices. At a finite-field fiber their
Satake structures are the normalized Weil structures of
Lemma~lem:normalized-weil-satake.


subsection{The characteristic restriction for texorpdfstring{SL_n}{SLn}}

The remaining geometric inputs of [cite] have four Lie-theoretic
hypotheses: (H1) a nondegenerate invariant symmetric form, also nondegenerate
on every Levi's Lie-algebra center; (H2) Chevalley restriction for every Levi;
(H3) scheme-theoretic semisimple centralizers that are Levis; and (H4)
containment of every nilpotent element, over every field extension, in the
nilradical of a parabolic defined over that field. We verify them to make the
characteristic restriction explicit.


For G=SL_n over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero or of
characteristic p>n, hypotheses (H1)--(H4) of cite[Theorem~1.1]{CT} hold.


Proof. 
The form kappa(X,Y)=tr(XY) is nondegenerate on
sl_n, since n is invertible. For a block Levi with block sizes
n_1,...,n_r, its restriction to the Lie-algebra center is the diagonal
form with entries n_i on
[
 {(z_i):textstylesum_i n_i z_i=0}.
]
This hyperplane is the orthogonal complement of (1,...,1), whose squared
length is n. All n_i and n are invertible, so the restricted form is
nondegenerate. This is (H1).

For (H2), apply block diagonalization on the dense regular-semisimple locus.
Block-symmetric polynomials in the eigenvalues lift by the block
characteristic polynomials. The Weyl-group order divides n!, which is
invertible; averaging shows that invariants on the total-trace-zero hyperplane
are restrictions of invariants on the full diagonal space. Block
diagonalization may be performed in the determinant-one Levi, by adjusting the
determinant with an element of the diagonal centralizer.

For (H3), the scheme-theoretic centralizer of a semisimple Lie-algebra element
is its block Levi, as follows directly from the matrix commutation equations.

For (H4), over every field extension, vanishing of the positive-degree
invariant polynomials says that the matrix is nilpotent. A basis adapted to its
kernel filtration puts it in a strictly triangular Lie algebra defined over
that field. It therefore lies in the nilradical of a rational Borel, which is
the required rational parabolic.


# An equivariant unramified eigencomplex

The first specialization begins in characteristic zero with an unramified
eigencomplex on a smooth projective curve. The geometric existence theorem
supplies the complex, but not its semilinear action. We obtain that action by
choosing the particular eigencomplex attached to a spectral skyscraper: its
spectral component is transport-stable and the skyscraper is recognized
intrinsically. A second rigidity argument then shows that any transport
isomorphism must respect the full eigenstructure.


Let C be a smooth projective connected curve of genus at least two over an
algebraically closed field K of characteristic zero. Let f be a semilinear
automorphism of C with an ample line bundle mathcal LcalL and an isomorphism
f^*mathcal LcalLsimeqmathcal L.calL. Let G=SL_n and Gd=PGL_n. Suppose
tau is a continuous Gd-local system on C, defined over a finite
extension of Q_ell, with Zariski-dense image, and equipped with an
isomorphism ucolon f^*tauxrightarrow{sim}tau.

Then there is a nonzero locally bounded constructible complex D_tau on
Bun_G(C), with nilpotent singular support, a full coherent Hecke
eigenstructure of eigenvalue tau, and an isomorphism
[
                 bcolon f^*D_tauxrightarrow{sim}D_tau
]
compatible with that eigenstructure and u. The object D_tau is compact in
the automorphic nilpotent category. The eigenstructure uses the relative
Satake normalization of Section~sec:conventions and is compatible with
the unit, convolution, permutations, and every fusion diagonal. The
isomorphisms b and u define compatible actions of Z on the eigencomplex
and on its eigenvalue.


Here f^* denotes transport on the curve and on its bundle stack, with the
coefficient field E fixed. We prove the proposition in three steps: identify
the spectral summand and its behavior under transport, recognize the
transported object without a shift, and prove uniqueness of the coherent
eigenstructure on that object.

# Arithmetic towers and equivariant gluing at a node


We build the parameter to which Proposition~prop:equivariant-unramified

punctured curve in characteristic zero, retaining its Frobenius action and its
full boundary homomorphism. Second we place this lift on one component of a
separating nodal curve and extend to an unramified parameter on a smooth
generic fiber; the gluing must preserve Frobenius and compactness of the
monodromy image. A ramified auxiliary component satisfies both at once.

## Perverse cohomology and removal of the enhancement

that monodromy will make the direct image nonzero; the second truncation then
gives the required perverse object.
