# $P=W$ in composite rank for fixed determinant




maketitle

Abstract: 
We prove the $P=W$ conjecture for the full rational cohomology of
fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces, in composite rank and
coprime degree, for smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two.
Combined with the known prime-rank cases, this gives the equality in every
coprime rank.


% ============================================================
# Overview: what is being proved, and why it is not obvious
% ============================================================

The two spaces.:  Fix a smooth projective complex curve $C$ of
genus $gge 2$. Fix a rank $nge 2$, a degree $d$ coprime to $n$, and a
line bundle $L$ of degree $d$ on $C$. Two very different spaces sit over $C$.

The Higgs moduli space $X$ parametrizes stable pairs $(E,theta)$:
$E$ is a rank-$n$ holomorphic vector bundle with $det Esimeq L$; $theta$
is a Higgs field, a traceless map $Eto E ⊗  K_C$ (where $K_C$ is
the canonical line bundle). The character variety $X^B$ parametrizes
flat connections, equivalently representations of the fundamental group of
$C$ with product of commutators $zeta I_n$, where
$zeta=exp(2pi√(-1),d/n)$. Nonabelian Hodge theory says $X$ and $X^B$
are diffeomorphic, so they have the same cohomology.

Two filtrations.:  On $H^*(X)$ the Hitchin map $h:Xto A$ (recording
the coefficients of $det(theta-t,I)$) defines the perverse Leray
filtration $P_k$. On $H^*(X^B)$ Deligne's theory of mixed Hodge structures
defines the weight filtration $W_j$. These come from completely
different geometry. The $P=W$ conjecture says they coincide after doubling
the perverse index:
[
  P_kH^m(X);=;W_{2k}H^m(X^B);=;W_{2k+1}H^m(X^B).
]

Why it is hard.:  In the unfixed determinant ($GL_n$) case
this is known in every rank [cite]. The fixed-determinant case splits
into the part invariant under the finite group $Gamma=Pic^0(C)[n]$ (acting
by tensor product), which reduces to $GL_n$, and the variant part,
which does not. Our proof does not throw away the variant part: it constructs
a single operator on the whole of $H^*(X)$ before taking any invariants.

The strategy in one paragraph.:  Both filtrations are controlled by
a degree-two raising operator $e$: cup product with a fixed class in $H^2$.
For each filtration, $e$ satisfies a hard Lefschetz theorem with the same
center $R=(n^2-1)(g-1)$. A classical lemma (proved in
Cref{sec:lefschetz}) says: if one degree-$(-2)$ operator $f$ lowers both
filtrations by two and satisfies the $sl_2$ relation
$[[f,e],e]=-2e$, then the two filtrations are equal, because both are rebuilt
from the eigenspaces of the same operator $[e,f]$. So the whole proof reduces
to producing such an $f$. That is done by a geometric construction: insert a
marked point with a logarithmic pole, move it over a finite cover of $C$,
read off monodromy, take a logarithm $N$, and set $fsim pr_*pi_*iota^*N^2
iota_*pi^*pr^*$.

Where each ingredient is proved.: 
[leftmargin=1.4em]
item Cref{sec:lefschetz}: normalizations, the class $e$, the abstract
      criterion (a common lowering operator determines the filtration).
item Cref{sec:betti}: hard Lefschetz for $e$ on the Betti side, on the
      whole cohomology.
item Cref{sec:diagram}: a bookkeeping device giving weight zero to the
      cochains of the moving-point curve, so that integrating over it does
      not shift weight.
item Cref{sec:logarithmic}: the moving logarithmic pole, restriction
      isomorphisms, and compatibility with nonabelian Hodge theory on the
      whole cohomology.
item Cref{sec:translation}: the elementary modification, and its
      logarithm $N$.
item Cref{sec:specialization}: the perverse bound, via nearby cycles.
item Cref{sec:commutator}: the double commutator $[[f',e],e]$.
item Cref{sec:conclusion}: assembly.


Standing conventions.:  Cohomology is with rational coefficients
unless $C$ is written. $R=(n^2-1)(g-1)$ and $b=tfrac{n(n-1)}2$ are fixed
throughout. The group $Gamma=Pic^0(C)[n]$ acts on $X$ by tensor product;
its averaging projector splits $H^*(X)$ into invariants and the variant part.
We prove the theorem for the composite integers $nge4$; the prime cases are
cite[Theorem~0.3]{MS2024}, and the two together cover every $nge2$.

# Lefschetz operators and the comparison criterion

% ============================================================

We fix once and for all the perverse normalization, exhibit the common
degree-two class $e$, and prove the abstract linear-algebra lemma that turns
"one common lowering operator" into "equal filtrations." From now on we
assume $nge4$ is composite; we say so again when coprimality is used.

## The normalized perverse filtration


With $R=(n^2-1)(g-1)$ one has $dim X=2R$, $dim A=R$, every fiber of $h$
has dimension $R$, and $dim(Xtimes_A X)-dim X=R$.


Proof. 
Riemann--Roch gives
$dim A=sum_{i=2}^n h^0(C,K_C^i)=sum_{i=2}^n(2i-1)(g-1)=R$.
The trace-free Higgs deformation complex has vanishing $H^0$ and $H^2$ by
stability and Serre duality, and Euler characteristic $-2R$; so each tangent
space of $X$ has dimension $2R$.

For the fibers, use Higgs-field scaling: $X$ is semiprojective, so every
scaling orbit closable at $∞$ has a limit, and the limit lies in the
proper fiber $h^{-1}(0)$ (all Hitchin coordinates have positive scaling
weight). At a fixed point the symplectic form pairs a tangent weight $w$ with
$1-w$; since weights are integers, the nonpositive part has exactly half the
tangent dimension, so every repelling stratum has dimension $R$. These strata
fill $h^{-1}(0)$, hence $dim h^{-1}(0)=R$. Pulling $h$ back along the scaling
curve toward $0$ and using upper semicontinuity of fiber dimension for the
proper map $h$ gives $dim h^{-1}(a)le R$ for every $a$; the dimension
inequality for a map from a smooth $2R$-fold onto an $R$-fold gives
$dim h^{-1}(a)ge R$ on every nonempty fiber. Thus every fiber is
$R$-dimensional and $dim(Xtimes_A X)=R+2R=3R$.


Put $K=Rh_*Q_X[R]$ and define the normalized perverse filtration by

 P_kH^m(X)=im!(
 H^{m-R}(A,{}^ptau_{le k}K)to H^m(X)).

The fiber bound forces the perverse cohomology of $Rh_*Q_X[2R]$ to live in
degrees $[-R,R]$ (support criterion gives the upper bound, Verdier duality
the lower); after the shift by $[-R]$, $K$ has perverse degrees in $[0,2R]$,
so

 P_{-1}H^*(X)=0,qquad P_{2R}H^*(X)=H^*(X).

We use the shift rule for a morphism $Kto K[s]$:

 {}^ptau_{le k}(K[s])=({}^ptau_{le k+s}K)[s],

so an actual morphism $Rh_*Q_Xto Rh_*Q_X[-2]$ lowers perversity by two.

## The degree-two class

The universal projective bundle on $Ctimes X$ determines the class
[
 chtilde_2(E)=ch_2(E)-(c_1(E)^2)/(2n)
 =frac1{2n}ch_2(End E),
]
the last expression being defined even without a chosen universal vector
bundle. Define

 e=int_Cchtilde_2(E)in H^2(X),

and write $e$ also for cup product with it.


For composite $nge4$:
$H^0(X)=Q$, $H^1(X)=0$, $H^2(X)=Q e$, and $ene0$.
Every ample class on $X$ is a nonzero multiple of $e$.


Proof. 
The group $Gamma=Pic^0(C)[n]$ acts; averaging splits $H^*$ into invariants
and the variant part $H^*_{var}$. Let $p$ be the smallest prime
divisor of $n$ and $c=n(n-n/p)(g-1)ge8$. cite[Proposition~1.4]{dCMS2022},
valid in any rank, gives $H^i_{var}=P_{i-c}H^i_{var}$, and
(norm:perverse-range) kills the variant part in degrees $0,1,2$.

The invariant part is the cohomology of the $PGL_n$ moduli space. Markman's
generation theorem cite[Theorem~7]{Markman2002}, in the projective-bundle form
of cite[Theorem~2.1(i)]{MS2024}, says this algebra is generated by the
K"unneth components of projective Chern characters of index $jge2$, of
degrees $2j,,2j-1,,2j-2$. So there is no degree-one generator and a single
possible degree-two generator, a nonzero normalization of $e$. Hence
$H^0=Q$, $H^1=0$, $H^2=Q e$ up to nonvanishing.

A Hitchin fiber is projective of positive dimension, so it contains a curve;
an ample class has positive degree on it and is nonzero in $H^2$, forcing
$ene0$ and the last claim.



Cup product by $e$ maps $P_kH^m(X)to P_{k+2}H^{m+2}(X)$, and for every
$ige0$ it induces an isomorphism

 e^i:Gr^P_{R-i}H^m(X)xrightarrow{sim}Gr^P_{R+i}H^{m+2i}(X).



Proof. 
The shift follows from (norm:shift-rule). Choose an ample class, also
relatively ample for the projective $h$. The decomposition theorem and
relative hard Lefschetz cite[Theorems~6.2.5, 6.2.10]{BBD1982} give
(norm:relative-hl) for that class (the perverse degrees of
$Rh_*Q_X[2R]$ are centered at $0$, and passing to $K$ centers them at $R$).
The decomposition theorem makes the perverse spectral sequence degenerate,
so sheaf-level isomorphisms give the associated-graded isomorphisms. Replace
the ample class by its nonzero multiple $e$.


## A common lowering operator determines the filtration

For a finite graded $V=⊕_kV_k$ and a degree-two $E$, say $E$ is
Lefschetz with center $r$ if $E^i:V_{r-i}xrightarrow{sim}V_{r+i}$
for all $ige0$. Cref{norm:relative-lefschetz} says the raiser on $Gr^P$ is
Lefschetz with center $R$.

[Comparison by a common lowering operator]

Let $H$ be finite-dimensional over a characteristic-zero field, with finite
exhaustive separated increasing integer filtrations $F_bullet,G_bullet$.
Suppose $e,finEnd(H)$ satisfy, for $A_bullet$ equal to $F_bullet$ or
$G_bullet$,
[
 e(A_k)⊆ A_{k+2},quad f(A_k)⊆ A_{k-2},
]
that the induced raiser on each associated graded is Lefschetz with the same
center $r$, and that

 [[f,e],e]=-2e,qquad [u,v]=uv-vu.

Then $F_k=G_k$ for all $k$. If $H$ carries a further grading respected by both
filtrations with $e,f$ of degrees $2,-2$, the equality is degreewise.


Proof. 
Fix one associated graded with raiser $E$. The Lefschetz decomposition writes
$E$ on primitive strings $v,Ev,...,E^ell v$ with $deg v=r-ell$. Put
[
 F_0(E^jv)=j(ell-j+1)E^{j-1}v,qquad
 H_0=k-r on grade k.
]
Direct computation gives $[E,F_0]=H_0$, $[H_0,E]=2E$, $[H_0,F_0]=-2F_0$, so
$(E,H_0,F_0)$ is an $sl_2$-triple and $[[F_0,E],E]=-2E$.

A degree-$(-2)$ endomorphism obeying this identity must equal $F_0$: on
$End(Gr^A H)$ the adjoint action labels degree $-2$ by $H_0$-weight $-2$,
and in any irreducible summand two raising steps inject the weight-$(-2)$
space into weight $2$. Thus $(ad,E)^2$ is injective on
degree-$(-2)$ endomorphisms, and applying it to $Gr^A(f)-F_0$ plus
(alg:double-commutator) gives $Gr^A(f)=F_0$.

Hence $h_0=[e,f]$ preserves both filtrations and acts by $k-r$ on grade $k$.
Then $(h_0-(k-r))A_k⊆ A_{k-1}$, and multiplying these factors from
$k=b$ down to $k=a$ over the range of nonzero grades gives
$prod_{k=a}^b(h_0-(k-r))=0$. The roots are distinct, so $h_0$ is semisimple
and every $h_0$-stable subspace splits along eigenspaces. An eigenvector first
entering $A_j$ has eigenvalue $j-r$, so

 A_k=⊕_{jle k}ker(h_0-(j-r)),

whose right side does not depend on the filtration. In the graded setting
$h_0$ has degree zero, so (alg:eigenspace-filtration) is degreewise.



Let $V,W$ be nonzero finite integer-graded spaces over a characteristic-zero
field, $E_V,E_W$ of degree two. If $E_V ⊗ 1+1 ⊗  E_W$ is Lefschetz
with center $r$ on $V ⊗  W$, then $E_V,E_W$ are Lefschetz with centers
$r_V,r_W$ and $r_V+r_W=r$.


Proof. 
Decompose into homogeneous Jordan strings (nilpotent, finite support). Each
tensor of two strings is a graded invariant direct summand with invariant
complement for the sum operator, and each Lefschetz isomorphism is block
diagonal, hence an isomorphism on each summand. For a string with extreme
grades $p,q$ call $(p+q)/2$ its center; the center of a tensor of two strings
is the sum of centers, and Lefschetz symmetry forces that sum to be $r$ for all
pairs. Fixing one factor's string forces all strings in the other factor to
share a center, and a Jordan string is Lefschetz about its own center.


We will apply Cref{alg:comparison} to total cohomology with $F_k=P_k$ and
$G_k=W_{2k}$, once Cref{sec:betti} proves $e$ is Betti-Lefschetz with center
$R$ and all odd weight-graded pieces vanish, and Cref{sec:translation,%
sec:specialization,sec:commutator} produce the one operator $f$ with both
lowering bounds and (alg:double-commutator).

# Curious hard Lefschetz on the full character variety

% ============================================================

Here we prove that $e$ is a Lefschetz operator for the weight filtration on
the whole cohomology of $X^B$, including every nontrivial
$Gamma$-character summand. The trick is to compute first on an ordered family
over a torus of eigenvalues, using a finite cover to reach the $GL_n$ toric
stratifications, and only at the very end specialize the eigenvalue parameter
to the scalar point and strip off flag and determinant factors.

Recall a mixed Hodge structure is Hodge--Tate if its odd weight-graded
pieces vanish and its weight-$2r$ piece has only Hodge type $(r,r)$.


The mixed Hodge structure on $H^*(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q)$ is Hodge--Tate. Under the
nonabelian-Hodge identification, $e$ has nonzero image in
$Gr^W_4H^2(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q)$, and for all $ige0$
[
 e^i:Gr^W_{2R-2i}H^m(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q)xrightarrow{sim}
      Gr^W_{2R+2i}H^{m+2i}(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q).
]
In particular $W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}$ on this cohomology, including all
$Gamma$-character summands after complexification.


## The ordered family and its scalar fiber

Let
[
 V=Bigl{(t_1,...,t_n)in(C^*)^n: textstyleprod_i t_i=1,
 prod_{iin I}t_ine1 for ∅ne I⊊{1,...,n}Bigr},
 qquad q=(zeta,...,zeta).
]
Then $V$ is a connected smooth open subset of a torus and $qin V$ since
$zeta$ has order $n$. Write $nu=prod_{j=1}^g[A_j,B_j]$. The fiber
$Y^{mathrm B}rmB_t$ parametrizes tuples $(A_1,B_1,...,A_g,B_g)inSL_n^{2g}$
together with a full flag preserved by $nu$, on whose ordered graded lines
$nu$ acts by $t_1,...,t_n$, modulo simultaneous conjugation; repeated
eigenvalues are allowed.

Every such tuple is irreducible: on a proper invariant subspace,
$detnu=1$ but the eigenvalues are a proper nonempty submultiset of the
$t_i$, contradicting the definition of $V$. So conjugation gives geometric
quotients by a free $PGL_n$ action (built from 'etale-local charts on
irreducible tuples; a full flag lets one complete the first line to a basis by
words in the matrices).


The morphism $Y^{mathrm B}rmBto V$ is smooth of relative dimension $2R+2b$.
There is a correspondence
[
 X^{mathrm B}rmBxleftarrow{ pi }Z^{mathrm B}rmB
 xrightarrow{ iota }Y^{mathrm B}rmB_q,
]
where $Z^{mathrm B}rmB$ imposes $nu=zeta I$ and $pi$ is the full flag bundle;
$iota$ is a regular embedding of codimension $b$ with normal bundle
$T_pi^*$. The operators $mathsf A=sfA=pi_*iota^*$ and $mathsf B=sfB=iota_*pi^*$
have degrees $-2b$ and $2b$ and satisfy

 mathsf AsfAmathsf B=(-1)^bsfB=(-1)^b n!,id.

(Covariant star denotes Gysin.)


Proof. 
The commutator product is a submersion at every irreducible tuple. For a pair
$a,b'$ set $c=ab'a^{-1}b'^{-1}$; the right-translated differential has terms
$(1-Ad_{ab'a^{-1}})u$ and $(Ad_a-Ad_c)v$. An
annihilating vector $z$ (trace pairing) is fixed by $ab'a^{-1}$ and satisfies
$Ad_{a^{-1}}z=Ad_{b'}z$; iterating over prefixes shows $z$
centralizes all matrices, so irreducibility makes it scalar and tracelessness
makes it zero.

Matrices in a Borel, with their flags, form a smooth space over the ordered
diagonal torus with $2b$-dimensional fibers; pulling back along the submersion
and quotienting gives smoothness with relative dimension
$2g(n^2-1)+2b-2(n^2-1)=2R+2b$. At $q$, the section $zeta^{-1}nu-I$ lands in
the nilradical bundle over the flag space, with zero locus $Z^{mathrm B}rmB$;
submersivity makes it regular, and the trace pairing identifies the
nilradical with $T_pi^*$. Self-intersection via the projection formula gives
$mathsf AsfAmathsf B(sfB(alpha)=pi_*(c_b(T_pi^*)pi^*alpha)
=(-1)^bchi(Fl_n)alpha=(-1)^b n!,alpha$. Everything descends through
$PGL_n$ and applies to full cohomology.


## Lifting the toric stratifications

Set $D_1=(C^*)^{2g}$ and $widehatwidehat Y^Y^{mathrm B}rmB=Y^{mathrm B}rmBtimes D_1$.
Scaling each of the $2g$ matrices by the corresponding scalar gives a finite
'etale map of degree $n^{2g}$

 widehatwidehat Y^Y^{mathrm B}rmBto Y^{mathrm B}rmB_{GL},

the target being the ordered family with matrices in $GL_n$: the source is a
genuine product because dividing by chosen determinant roots returns matrices
in $SL_n$ without changing commutators or flags.

We use Mellit's toric stratifications [cite]. To identify the
family, take two punctures with first monodromy $zeta^{-1}I$ and last
monodromy $zetanu^{-1}$, whose ordered eigenvalues are $(zeta t_i^{-1})_i$.
The product relation reads $nuzeta^{-1}Icdotzetanu^{-1}=I$, and the
genericity condition of cite[Definition~4.9]{Mellit2025} is exactly the
subproduct condition defining $V$; the identity last monodromy is $q$, and the
ordering condition is automatic for a scalar first monodromy and a
distinct-eigenvalue last monodromy.

Two features of these stratifications are used. First, at a parameter with
distinct $t_i$, an auxiliary rank-$b$ vector bundle over
$Y^{mathrm B}rmB_{GL,t}$ has a finite stratification refined into
vector bundles over products of tori and affine spaces; Mellit's degree-two
class then gives compact-support curious Lefschetz on every final stratum,
with the same middle actual weight $delta+2b$ ($delta=dim
Y^{mathrm B}rmB_{GL,t}$), and removing the vector-bundle shift gives
middle weight $delta$ cite[Theorem~6.14, Section~7.4, Theorem~7.5]{Mellit2025}.
Second, over all of $V$, an auxiliary affine bundle of rank $b$ has a finite
stratification refined into vector bundles over
$mathbb A^abbA^atimes(C^*)^stimes V$, projecting to $V$
cite[proof of Proposition~8.7]{Mellit2025}. These statements concern the
final torus--affine refinements.


Pulling the preceding bundles and stratifications back along
(betti:cover):

item Every fiber of $widehatwidehat Y^Y^{mathrm B}rmBto V$ and of $Y^{mathrm B}rmBto V$
has Hodge--Tate cohomology. For $widehatwidehat Y^Y^{mathrm B}rmB$ at distinct
eigenvalues, the pulled-back class satisfies curious hard Lefschetz on
ordinary cohomology with half-weight center $delta/2=R+b+g$.
item The cohomology sheaves of both ordinary and compact-support integral
direct images over $V$ are locally constant and finitely generated, and
ordinary direct image commutes with restriction to every fiber; the same holds
for $Y^{mathrm B}rmBto V$.



Proof. 
Consider a final stratum $F=(C^*)^stimesmathbb A^a$,bbA^a$, or a vector bundle over
it; its homotopy type is a torus. Each connected component
$widetilde F$ of a finite 'etale preimage corresponds to a finite-index
subgroup of $Z^s$, and $H^*(F,Q)to H^*(widetilde F,Q)$ is an isomorphism
of exterior algebras, hence of mixed Hodge structures (algebraic map). Both
spaces are smooth of the same dimension, and finite trace plus Poincar'e
duality give the compact-support analogue; these isomorphisms commute with the
class.

Apply componentwise, then use the finite closed filtration and the strictness
of mixed Hodge morphisms cite[Theorem~2.3.5]{Deligne1971} on compact-support
localization sequences; the five lemma passes the Lefschetz isomorphisms and
Hodge--Tate property to the whole auxiliary space. The Thom isomorphism shifts
degree and weight by $2b$; compact-support middle weight drops from
$delta+2b$ to $delta$, and Poincar'e duality ($wmapsto2delta-w$) gives the
same middle weight $delta$, i.e. center $delta/2$. The relative product
stratification handles repeated eigenvalues the same way.

For integral local constancy, take $Usubset V$ small contractible analytic;
a finite cover of $Ftimes U$ is pulled back from one of $F$ (since
$pi_1(Ftimes U)=pi_1(F)$), and the same holds for a vector bundle over $F$
(homotopy equivalent to its base; equivalently use the complex-orientation
Thom isomorphism). So each covered stratum has locally constant finitely
generated compact-support sheaves on $U$; localization triangles pass this up
a finite stratification, and the auxiliary affine trace removes the shift.

For the covered smooth family $f$ of relative dimension $delta$,
$K=Rf_!Z$ is locally perfect (bounded, finitely generated), so smooth Verdier
duality gives
$Rf_*Zsimeq RHom(K,Z_V)[-2delta]$; the right side is locally perfect, so
stalks commute with the duality, and compact-support base change plus the
fiberwise cup--trace pairing identify the stalk with
$RGamma(f^{-1}(t),Z)$, verifying ordinary base change. Passing to
$Y^{mathrm B}rmB$: the projection $p:Y^{mathrm B}rmBtimes D_1to Y^{mathrm B}rmB$
splits off a section for ordinary direct images, and the Gysin map of the
closed section followed by integration over $D_1$ splits it for compact
supports. Thus local constancy and base change descend, and the algebraic
splitting transfers Hodge--Tate-ness to $Y^{mathrm B}rmB$.


## The scalar summand and the determinant factor

For a smooth algebraic family with lisse ordinary direct images satisfying
fiberwise base change, mixed Hodge module direct image gives admissible
graded-polarizable variations of mixed Hodge structure on the local systems
(constructed in Proposition~diag:variation); parallel transport
preserves the weight filtration, and cup products are parallel. Applied to
Cref{betti:finite-cover}, transport a Lefschetz class from a
distinct-eigenvalue fiber along a path in the connected $V$ to $q$, giving
[
 omegainGr^W_4H^2(widehatwidehat Y^Y^{mathrm B}rmB_q,C)
]
with center $R+b+g$. (A choice of path suffices.)

Take the product of Cref{betti:geometry} with $D_1$, setting
$widehatwidehat X^X^{mathrm B}rmB=X^{mathrm B}rmBtimes D_1$,
$widehatwidehat Z^Z^{mathrm B}rmB=Z^{mathrm B}rmBtimes D_1$. The flag bundle has rational
algebraic Leray--Hirsch classes: ratios of ordered graded lines descend for
the universal projective bundle and their first Chern classes generate the
flag cohomology over $Q$ (differences of Chern roots generate rationally
because the roots sum to zero on a fiber), and polynomial representatives give
global classes. These extra degree-two classes have weight two, hence
[
 pi^*:Gr^W_4H^2(widehatwidehat X^X^{mathrm B}rmB,C)
 xrightarrow{sim}Gr^W_4H^2(widehatwidehat Z^Z^{mathrm B}rmB,C),
]
and there is a unique $xi$ on the left with $iota^*omega=pi^*xi$ on
weight-gradeds. By the projection formula $mathsf A,sfA,mathsf B$sfB$ intertwine
multiplication by $omega$ and $xi$ on weight-gradeds; their actual weight
shifts are $-2b,2b$. Since (betti:split) exhibits the image of $mathsf B$sfB$
as a graded stable summand with stable complement $kermathsf A$,sfA$, each
Lefschetz isomorphism for $omega$ restricts to one on the summand. The
half-weight shift of $mathsf B$sfB$ is $b$, so $xi$ has center $R+g$ on
$H^*(widehatwidehat X^X^{mathrm B}rmB,C)$, whose cohomology is thus a Tate-twisted
summand of Hodge--Tate cohomology, hence Hodge--Tate.

Remove $D_1$: Cref{norm:low-cohomology} and nonabelian Hodge give
$H^0(X^{mathrm B}rmB)=Q$, $H^1(X^{mathrm B}rmB)=0$, so K"unneth gives
$xi=xi_X+xi_D$ with $xi_XinGr^W_4H^2(X^{mathrm B}rmB,C)$ and
$xi_Din H^2(D_1,C)$. Cref{alg:tensor} shows both factors have common
Lefschetz centers summing to $R+g$; $H^*(D_1)$ is an exterior algebra on $2g$
generators of degree one and weight two, with half-weight grades $0$ to $2g$,
hence center $g$. Thus $xi_X$ has center $R$, and projection/section again
give $X^{mathrm B}rmB$ Hodge--Tate cohomology.

Since $H^0(X^{mathrm B}rmB)ne0$ and $R>0$, $xi_X$ is nonzero; by
Cref{norm:low-cohomology} $H^2(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q)=Q e$, so the image of $e$ in
$Gr^W_4$ is nonzero and a nonzero multiple of $xi_X$ (and $ein W_4$ since
$X^{mathrm B}rmB$ is smooth). Rescaling preserves Lefschetz isomorphisms, which
hold over $Q$ since they become isomorphisms over $C$. This proves
Cref{betti:lefschetz}, with no step taking $Gamma$-invariants.

# Weights for flat bundles of algebraic families

% ============================================================

Consider a product $Stimes X^{mathrm B}rmB$ with $S$ a closed oriented smooth
surface. The moving-point construction needs integration over $S$ to lower
degree by two while preserving weight. So on this product we give the
K"unneth factor $H^a(S,Q)$ pure weight zero and type $(0,0)$ in every degree,
and keep Deligne's mixed Hodge structure on $H^*(X^{mathrm B}rmB,Q)$; then
integration over $S$ acts on the first factor without a Tate twist. Even for
an algebraic curve $S$ this differs from its ordinary Deligne weights. Because
the moving-puncture flag spaces are not products, we extend the convention to
bundles whose transition maps are locally constant algebraic automorphisms of
a fiber family. A finite diagram of algebraic fibers carries the mixed Hodge
structures together with parallel transport and proper Gysin maps. The
simplicial idea goes back to Deligne cite[Section~8.3]{Deligne1974} and was
used by Shende for tautological classes cite[proof of the main
Theorem]{Shende2017}; we supply the relative construction and Gysin
compatibility.

## The finite diagram

Let $V$ be a smooth connected complex variety and $p:Hto V$ an algebraic
morphism of finite type. Assume the cohomology sheaves of $Rp_*Z_H$ are
bounded, locally constant, and finitely generated, and that the natural
ordinary base-change maps

 i_t^*Rp_*Z_Hto RGamma(H_t,Z)

are isomorphisms for all $tin V$ (these concern ordinary direct image;
smoothness alone does not give them for a nonproper morphism).

For a compact smooth manifold $S$, a flat bundle of the family $Hto V$
over $S$ is a space $mathscr HscrHto Stimes V$ with local identifications
$mathscr H|_scrH|_{U_itimes V}simeq U_itimes H$ whose transition maps are
$(s,h)mapsto(s,g_{ij}(h))$ with $g_{ij}$ an algebraic automorphism over $V$
locally constant in $s$, satisfying the cocycle identity. Write
$mathscr H_t$scrH_t$ for the total space over $S$ at $t$ (it includes $S$ and need
not be algebraic). A map of flat bundles is the identity on $S$ times an
algebraic map of fiber families in simultaneous flat trivializations.

Choose a finite good cover $mathcal U=calU={U_i}$ refining a flat trivializing
cover (all $U_I=∩_{iin I}U_i$ contractible), a trivialization on each
$U_I$; inclusions give a finite diagram of copies $H_Ito V$ and algebraic
isomorphisms over $V$, indexed by the poset of nonempty intersections ordered
toward restriction. Using the enhanced bounded derived category of mixed Hodge
modules with diagram-compatible six operations cite[Section~2.1,
Theorem~2.1.1, Remark~2.1.2, Proposition~4.2.6]{TubachStacks2025}, and writing
$Q_T^H$ for the constant mixed Hodge module complex without dimension shift,
define the derived diagram

 K_{mathscr H}scrH=holim_I,Rp_{I*}Q_{H_I}^Hquadin D^bMHM(V).

The index poset has finite-dimensional nerve, so this is a finite limit and
stays bounded; (diag:complex) uses the whole derived diagram, not only
cohomology of its terms.

[Diagram weights]
Under these hypotheses, $H^j(mathscr H_t,scrH_t,Q)$ carries a graded-polarizable
mixed Hodge structure, functorial for maps of flat bundles algebraic in flat

then so is $H^j(mathscr H_t,scrH_t,Q)$. We call its weight filtration the
diagram weight filtration.


Proof. 
Open-cover descent computes the ordinary direct image of $mathscr HscrHto V$ from
the spaces $U_Itimes H_I$; projection to $H_I$ has contractible ($U_I$) fibers
over $V$ and is quasi-isomorphic on direct images, commuting with transitions,
so the derived limit identifies the rational realization of $K_{mathscr H}scrH$
with the ordinary direct image (same integrally).

A finite limit of bounded complexes with locally constant cohomology has
locally constant finitely generated cohomology; point pullback commutes with
finite limits, and termwise base change is an isomorphism by
(diag:basechange), with the corresponding mixed Hodge module maps
isomorphisms by conservativity of rational realization. So $i_t^*K_{mathscr

underlying cohomology is $H^*(mathscr H_t,scrH_t,Q)$.

perverse truncations shows the fiber is $H^j(i_t^*K_{mathscr H}scrH)$ (a smooth
perverse object has only fiber degree $-v$).

Finally, mixed Hodge structures with weight gradeds of Hodge type $(r,r)$ only
are closed under subobjects, quotients, and extensions; the cone long exact
sequence then makes complexes with Hodge--Tate cohomology closed under finite
limits, giving the last assertion. Algebraic diagram maps give functoriality,
and a common good refinement compares choices of cover.


The construction preserves the cup product: each algebraic direct image of the
constant complex has its functorial product preserved by transitions, the
derived limit inherits it, and under the open-cover comparison it is the
ordinary cup product on $H^*(mathscr H_t,scrH_t,Q)$. So parallel transport preserves
both the ring and its weight filtration.

## Products and proper Gysin maps

[Weight shifts]
The diagram weight filtrations satisfy:

item For $Stimes M$ with $M$ algebraic, the K"unneth isomorphism
identifies the structure with that on $H^*(S,Q) ⊗  H^*(M,Q)$, where
every $H^a(S,Q)$ is pure of weight zero and type $(0,0)$ and $H^*(M,Q)$ has

projection pullback and integration over $S$ preserve weight, the latter with
degree $-d_S$.
item Pullbacks by maps of flat bundles preserve weight.
item For a proper map of flat bundles $u:mathscr H_tscrH_ttomathscr H'_t$scrH'_t$ with

W_{a+2c}H^{j+2c}(mathscr H'_t)$.scrH'_t)$. If $S$ is oriented, this is the Gysin map for
the induced orientations.



Proof. 
For a product the diagram is constant with derived limit

## Finite monodromy on weight gradeds


Assume the Hodge--Tate hypothesis of Cref{diag:variation}. The combined
monodromy on all $Gr^W_aH^j(mathscr H_t,scrH_t,Q)$ has finite image. For a loop with
cohomology monodromy $T$ there is $M>0$ with $T^M$ unipotent and

 N=tfrac1Mlog(T^M) satisfying N(W_a)subset W_{a-2}.

The logarithm is a finite polynomial, and $N$ is a degree-zero derivation of
the cohomology ring.


Proof. 
Each nonzero weight graded is a polarizable variation of pure type $(r,r)$ and
weight $2r$; after the conventional sign its flat polarization is positive
definite on the real part. Taking the torsion-free quotient of the integral
local system and intersecting with the rational weight sub-local systems gives
an invariant full lattice in each rational graded piece (saturated, as the
subspaces are rational). Automorphisms of a lattice preserving a positive
definite form form a finite group (columns have bounded norm in a fixed basis),
so each graded monodromy image is finite; finitely many degrees and weights
give the combined finiteness.

Choose $M$ killing the specified loop on all weight gradeds; then
$(T^M-1)W_a⊆ W_{a-1}=W_{a-2}$ for even $a$ (and identically for odd $a$
since consecutive odd/even steps coincide). $T^M-1$ is nilpotent (finite
filtration), and its finite logarithm has the same lowering bound.

Parallel transport preserves products, so $exp(kMN)=T^{Mk}$ is a ring
automorphism for every $kge0$; expanding the polynomial identity in $k$ and
comparing linear coefficients gives the derivation property, and monodromy
preserves degree.

with a moving marked point and its flag correspondence, and identify the scalar
unflagged bundle as a flat product, so that Cref{diag:gysin} controls
push-pull and Cref{diag:finite} supplies the weight-lowering logarithm.

# A common cohomological operator

% ============================================================

We turn an elementary modification of Higgs bundles into monodromy of Betti
spaces. All cohomology identifications here are the restriction maps of
Cref{log:restriction}, which pin down one operator before we bound its effect
on either filtration.

## Modification of two eigenlines

Choose $vin√(-1)R^n$ with sum zero, distinct entries, and no vanishing
proper nonempty partial sum (generic: the excluded conditions are finitely many
proper real hyperplanes). Set $ell=(1,-1,0,...,0)$. At a logarithmic operator
with distinct residue eigenvalues, a positive elementary modification allows a
simple pole in a chosen residue eigenline; a negative one takes the kernel of the
projection to that eigenline at $x$. With the convention that a positive shift
replaces a local generator $s$ by $z^{-1}s$, the $lambda$-Leibniz rule changes
the residue eigenvalue by $-lambda$; a negative shift changes it by $+lambda$.
We perform the shifts of $ell$ successively: first the positive modification on
the first labeled eigenline, then (after reselecting the labeled eigenspaces of
the new residue) the negative modification on the second. The ordered eigenspaces
of the final residue define the final flag.


The two modifications define a continuous family of isomorphisms

 F_a:Y_{a,v}to Y_{a,v-aell},qquad 0le ale1.

At $a=0$ they give an algebraic automorphism of $Y_{0,v}$; more generally they
give an automorphism over the punctured Higgs line $(lambda,u)=(0,zv)$, $zne0$,
preserving the logarithmic Hitchin map. At $a=1$ the Riemann--Hilbert
identifications make $F_1$ the identity on the underlying Betti space.


Proof. 
For a single modification choose a local frame adapted to the current residue
eigenspaces: off-diagonal coefficients involving the chosen eigenline vanish at
the pole, so the modified lattice is preserved by the operator, with the
prescribed new residue eigenvalues. Their imaginary parts stay distinct along
(trans:path), even after the first step, so we can reselect before the
second modification. The two-step composite preserves the determinant and its
operator (the shifts sum to zero), and its inverse does the opposite single
modifications in reverse with reselection between; both vary algebraically where
successive residues are distinct.

Stability is not automatic. On the Higgs line with $zne0$, a proper invariant
saturated subbundle would have a trace Higgs differential with a single possible
simple pole, whose residue $zsum_{iin I}v_i$ (proper nonempty $I$) is nonzero,
against the residue theorem; modified bundles obey the same argument. Distinct
residue eigenvalues split the local operator into formal eigenline summands;
shifting and shifting back proves invertibility.

For $a>0$ divide by $a$: a proper invariant monodromy subspace would have
determinant monodromy one around the puncture, but the imaginary part of the sum
of its exponents is $a^{-1}sum_{iin I}Im,v_ine0$; and differences of
exponents have nonzero imaginary part, so no integral resonance occurs, keeping
inverse modifications valid and stable.

The characteristic polynomial on the Higgs line is unchanged away from the pole,
hence unchanged as a meromorphic polynomial. Modifications change only the
logarithmic lattice at the puncture; at $a=1$ the exponent changes are integers,
so the meromorphic flat bundle and ordered monodromy eigenlines are unchanged,
which is exactly the endpoint identification.

# The translation class and the double commutator

## The characteristic class under translation

## The flag integral


Let $pi:Fl(V)to B$ be the full flag bundle of a rank-$n$ complex vector bundle
with formal Chern roots $w_1,...,w_n$. For a polynomial $F$ in the first Chern
classes of the ordered flag lines,
[

 =sum_{sigmainmathfrak S_n}frakS_nF(w_{sigma(1)},...,w_{sigma(n)}).
]
If $sum_iell_i=0$, then

 sum_{sigmainmathfrak S_n}frakS_n(sum_iell_iw_{sigma(i)})^2
 =n(n-2)!|ell|^2(sum_iw_i^2-((sum_iw_i)^2)/(n)).

Both identities, for common-translation-invariant expressions, hold for the
associated flag bundle of a principal $PGL_n$-bundle with rational coefficients.


Proof. 
Iterating the projective pushforward (sum over roots with denominator the
other-root differences) along the flag tower gives a sum over ordered roots with
denominator the tangent Euler class; multiplication by $c_b(T_pi)$ cancels it,
giving the first identity. It may be proven after passing to a splitting space
and inverting root differences, the result being a polynomial identity in the
universal Chern classes; the complex tangent Euler class fixes orientation signs.

For (calc:permutation), a fixed root occurs $(n-1)!$ times at a fixed
position, and a fixed ordered pair of distinct roots occurs $(n-2)!$ times at two
prescribed positions. With $sum_{ine j}ell_iell_j=-|ell|^2$, expanding the
square gives
[
 (n-1)!|ell|^2textstylesum_iw_i^2-(n-2)!|ell|^2sum_{ine j}w_iw_j
 =n(n-2)!|ell|^2(sum_iw_i^2-((sum_iw_i)^2)/(n)).
]
Finally, rational pullback from $BPGL_n$ to $BGL_n$ is injective (symmetric
polynomials in root differences into all roots on a maximal torus), so
common-translation-invariant characteristic identities can be checked after it.


## The common lowering operator


Let $delta=deg(x:Sto C)$. The operator $f'$ of (trans:fprime) satisfies

 [[f',e],e]=8(-1)^b n(n-2)!delta,e.

Consequently

 f=frac{(-1)^{b+1}}{4n(n-2)!delta},f'

has degree $-2$, lowers perversity by two and Betti weights by four, and satisfies
$[[f,e],e]=-2e$.


Proof. 
Work on the scalar correspondence at $lambda=0$. The restriction of
$e_{mathcal Y}calY$ along $iota:Z_0↪ Y_{0,0}$ equals
$pi^*pr_0^*e$ (the universal projective class is unchanged by the line twist
$J$), so the maps outside $N_{0,0}^2$ in $f'$ intertwine multiplication by $e$ and
$e_{mathcal Y}calY$.

For a degree-zero derivation $N$ and an even class $a$, with $m_a$ multiplication,
$[N,m_a]=m_{N(a)}$, and direct expansion gives
[
 [[N^2,m_a],m_a]=2m_{N(a)^2}
]
(the first commutator is $m_{N^2(a)}+2m_{N(a)}N$; the first term commutes with
$m_a$, the second contributes $2m_{N(a)^2}$). By Cref{calc:translation-formula},

 [[f',e],e]=2pr_{0,*}mathsf A_0sfA_0,(y^2cup-),mathsf B_0sfB_0pr_0^*.


Since $N_iota=T_pi^*$, self-intersection replaces $iota^*iota_*$ by
$c_b(T_pi^*)cup-=(-1)^b c_b(T_pi)cup-$. For a class pulled back from $X$, the
projection formula and Cref{calc:flag-sum} reduce the flag integral to
[
 sum_{sigmainmathfrak S_n}frakS_n(w_{sigma(1)}-w_{sigma(2)})^2
 =2n(n-2)!(sum_iw_i^2-((sum_iw_i)^2)/(n))
 =4n(n-2)!,chtilde_2(E_{x(s)}),
]
where $E_{x(s)}$ is the universal projective data restricted to the graph of $x$
(the normalized expression is defined without a genuine universal bundle).

Under $O_0simeq Stimes X$ this class is the pullback of
$chtilde_2(E)$ from $Ctimes X$ along $xtimesid_X$; the twist $J$
contributes nothing. Integration along $S$ multiplies integration along $C$ by
$delta$ (from $x_*[S]=delta[C]$, or the $C$-degree-two K"unneth term):
[
 pr_{0,*}chtilde_2(E_{x(s)})=delta,e,
]
other curve degrees integrating to zero. Combining with the factor $2$ in
(calc:middle-commutator) and the self-intersection sign gives
(calc:scalar). The integer $delta$ is positive ($x$ a nonempty finite
'etale cover of connected curves), so (calc:f) is a nonzero rational
rescaling giving the stated double commutator; $f'$ has degree $-2$ (flag shifts
cancel, integration over $S$ is degree $-2$), and the filtration bounds are
Cref{spec:perverse-lowering} and Cref{trans:weights}, unchanged by rescaling.

# Equality of the filtrations

% ============================================================


For every smooth projective complex curve $C$ of genus $gge2$, every composite
integer $nge4$, every integer $d$ coprime to $n$, and every $LinPic^d(C)$,
[
 P_kH^m(X^B,Q)=W_{2k}H^m(X^B,Q)=W_{2k+1}H^m(X^B,Q)
]
for all $m,kge0$. The equality concerns the entire cohomology, including every
nontrivial $Gamma$-character summand after complexification.


Proof. [Proof of Cref{thm:main}]
First take $L=mathcal O_C(dx_0)$.calO_C(dx_0)$. Cref{norm:relative-lefschetz} gives hard
Lefschetz for cup product by $e$ on $Gr^P H^*(X)$ (grading shift two, center
$R$); Cref{sec:betti} gives the same on $Gr^W H^*(X^B)$ with actual weight
$2k$ as grade $k$, and all odd weight-graded pieces vanish.

Let $delta=deg(Sto C)>0$ and $f=dfrac{(-1)^{b+1}}{4n(n-2)!,delta}f'$.
Cref{trans:weights} and Cref{spec:perverse-lowering} show $f$ lowers the
half-weight filtration and the perverse filtration by two, and
Cref{calc:double-commutator} gives $[[f,e],e]=-2e$. Apply
Cref{alg:comparison} to $P_k$ and $W_{2k}$: $P_kH^m(X)=W_{2k}H^m(X^B)$ in every
cohomological degree, and vanishing of odd weight-graded pieces gives
$W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}$.

For arbitrary $LinPic^d(C)$, choose $MinPic^0(C)$ with
$M^nsimeq L ⊗ mathcal O_C(-dx_0)$;calO_C(-dx_0)$; tensoring by $M$ identifies the Higgs
spaces over the Hitchin base, and Cref{log:nah} shows this identification
transports the full Betti weight filtration as well.



With $gge2$ and coprime $n,d$, and any $LinPic^d(C)$, nonabelian Hodge theory
identifies
$P_kH^m(X,Q)=W_{2k}H^m(X^B,Q)=W_{2k+1}H^m(X^B,Q)$ for all $m,k$, on full
rational cohomology, including its $Pic^0(C)[n]$-variant part.

Proof. 
Composite ranks are Cref{thm:main}; prime ranks are
cite[Theorem~0.3]{MS2024}.


[Endoscopic weight compatibility]
Keep $C,n,d,L$ as above. Put $Gamma=Pic^0(C)[n]$, let $gammainGamma$ have
order $m$, and let $kappainwidehatGamma$,
$kappa(gamma')=<gamma,gamma'>_Gamma$ be the character from the
Weil pairing cite[Section~1.3]{MSEndoscopy2021}. Let $pi:C'to C$ be the
associated cyclic 'etale cover, $r=n/m$, and
$d_gamma=codim_{mathcal A^calA^{K_C}}mathcal A_calA_gamma^{K_C}$ for the
endoscopic Hitchin base. For $D=K_C$ and $D'=pi^*K_C=K_{C'}$, let
$mathfrak p_frakp_kappa^B$ be the nonabelian-Hodge-transported operator of
cite[Section~5.4]{MSEndoscopy2021}. Writing
$mathcal Mtilde_calMtilde_{r,d}^B(C')$ for the full degree-$d$ twisted $GL_r$
character variety and $mathcal M_calM_{n,L}^B(C)=X^B$, for all $i,kge0$,
[
 mathfrak p_frakp_kappa^B(W_{2k}H^i(mathcal Mtilde_calMtilde_{r,d}^B(C'),C))
 =W_{2k+2d_gamma}H^{i+2d_gamma}(mathcal M_calM_{n,L}^B(C),C)_kappa,
]
the subscript selecting the summand with $gamma'^*alpha=kappa(gamma')alpha$.

Proof. 
Since $rmid n$, $gcd(r,d)=1$, and 'etale Riemann--Hurwitz gives
$g(C')=m(g-1)+1ge2$. The all-rank $GL_r$ theorem cite[Theorem~0.2]{MS2024}
identifies $W_{2k}$ with $P_k$ on the source (also for $r=1$). Cref{cor:all-rank}
identifies $P_{k+d_gamma}$ with $W_{2k+2d_gamma}$ on the target after
complexification and passage to the $Gamma$-$kappa$ summand (the $Gamma$-action
preserves both filtrations, and the target identification is $Gamma$-equivariant).
With the common defect normalization, cite[Theorem~5.4]{MSEndoscopy2021} gives
the exact image of $P_kH^i$ as $P_{k+d_gamma}H^{i+2d_gamma}_kappa$. Writing
$eta_s,eta_t$ for the source and target identifications, the definition
$mathfrak p_frakp_kappa^B=eta_t^{-1}mathfrak p_frakp_kappaeta_s$ gives the claim; its
nonzero scalar ambiguity does not affect images.


The standard implication from $P=W$ to perverse multiplicativity
cite[Section~0.4.3, equation~(10)]{dCMSJAMS2022} now applies to full
fixed-determinant cohomology.

[Multiplicativity of the perverse filtration]

In the setting of Cref{cor:all-rank}, with normalization
(norm:perverse-filtration), for all $i,jge0$ and $a,binZ$,
[
 P_aH^i(X,Q)smile P_bH^j(X,Q)⊆ P_{a+b}H^{i+j}(X,Q).
]
For isotypic parts indexed by $chi,psiinwidehatGamma$ (complexified),
[
 (P_aH^i(X,C))_chismile(P_bH^j(X,C))_psi
 ⊆(P_{a+b}H^{i+j}(X,C))_{chipsi},
]
including trivial and distinct characters, with the same normalized $P$ on every
character summand.

Proof. 
The nonabelian-Hodge diffeomorphism induces a graded ring isomorphism
$eta:H^*(X^B,Q)to H^*(X,Q)$ cite[Sections~2,~5]{HT2003}. Cup product is a
morphism of mixed Hodge structures cite[Corollaire~(8.2.11)]{Deligne1974}, so it
sends $W_{2a}H^i(X^B,Q)smile W_{2b}H^j(X^B,Q)$ into
$W_{2(a+b)}H^{i+j}(X^B,Q)$; transport by $eta$ and the all-rank $P=W$ equality
give the first assertion. The filtration is $Gamma$-stable and
$gamma^*(alphasmilebeta)=gamma^*alphasmilegamma^*beta$ multiplies the
eigencharacters, giving the second.
