The entropy photon number of an $n$-mode bosonic state is the mean photon number per mode of the product of $n$ identical one-mode thermal states carrying the same total entropy. Guha, Erkmen, and Shapiro conjectured that passive beam-splitter mixing of independent inputs cannot push this quantity above the weighted average of the input values. The conjecture is the bosonic counterpart of the classical entropy power inequality introduced by Shannon in 1948 (Bell System Technical Journal 27, 379--423 and 623--656), with Fisher-information proofs by Stam in 1959 (Information and Control 2, 101--112) and Blachman in 1965 (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 11, 267--271).

Earlier work placed the entropy photon number among quantum entropy power bounds. Konig and Smith (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 60 (2014), 1536--1548) proved a quantum entropy power inequality with a linear entropy bound and a balanced-beam-splitter exponential bound. De Palma, Mari, and Giovannetti (Nature Photonics 8 (2014), 958--964) proved the exponential bound for every transmissivity, and De Palma, Mari, Lloyd, and Giovannetti (Physical Review A 91 (2015), 032320) treated general linear mixing of several independent groups. De Palma and Trevisan (Communications in Mathematical Physics 360 (2018), 639--662) gave a finite-energy conditional treatment. The Gaussian optimizer theorem of Giovannetti, Holevo, and Garcia-Patron (Communications in Mathematical Physics 334 (2015), 1553--1571) settled the unconstrained minimum-output entropy conjecture. At fixed input entropy, De Palma, Trevisan, and Giovannetti proved passive rearrangement (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 62 (2016), 2895--2906), the sharp one-mode attenuator bound (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 63 (2017), 728--737), the sharp one-mode phase-covariant bound (Physical Review Letters 118 (2017), 160503), and a multimode extension (arXiv:1705.00499). De Palma (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 65 (2019), 5959--5968) proved the sharp constrained bound for multimode entanglement-breaking Gaussian attenuators and amplifiers with thermal environments. Beigi and Rahimi-Keshari (Annales Henri Poincare 26 (2025), 2737--2778) proved a meta logarithmic-Sobolev inequality for phase-covariant Gaussian channels, and Beigi and Mehrabi (Communications in Mathematical Physics 407 (2026), article 199) proved monotonicity of the von Neumann entropy under quantum convolution. The direct two-input form remained open.

Lemma (compactness and entropy continuity at fixed energy). For each finite $Ege0$, the set of states with $Trrho Nle E$ is compact in trace norm. Entropy is continuous on this set and obeys
