maketitle
Abstract: 
We construct a finitely generated torsion-free group G whose reduced
Baum--Connes assembly map has an integral kernel class of infinite order.
The proof has three layers: a deterministic labeled graph with short-word
uniqueness, a probabilistic selection of expanding permutation tables and
diffuse Heisenberg voltages, and a spectral argument in a reduced
group algebra which cancels the rank-zero Bott class of an embedded torus.
The final step extracts a finite witness from a compatible accuracy sequence.

# Notation and conventions

All group C^*-algebras and all K-groups are complex. Commutators are
[a,b]=aba^{-1}b^{-1}. For a discrete group Λ we write
Cr(Λ) for the reduced group C^*-algebra, i.e. the norm
closure of C[Λ] acting by the left regular representation on
ell^2(Λ). For a locally compact space X, K_*(X) denotes
topological K-homology with compact support, i.e. the directed colimit
of K_*(X') over finite subcomplexes X'⊆ X. When Λ is
torsion-free, the classifying space for proper actions EΛ
coincides with BΛ, so the coefficient-free assembly map is
[
  mu_Λ^rcolon K_*(BΛ)to K_*(Cr(Λ)).
]
On C_r^*(Z^2)cong C(mathbb T^2)bbT^2) we fix the spin Dirac class
[D_{mathbb T^2}bbT^2]in K_0(mathbb T^2)bbT^2) of the oriented torus and the
rank-zero Bott generator beta=[mathsf p]-[e_∞]sfp]-[e_∞]in K_0(C(mathbb T^2))bbT^2))
where mathsf psfp is the rank-one Bott projection and e_∞=diag(0,1).
The reduced assembly map sends [D_{mathbb T^2}bbT^2] to (a choice of sign of) beta.

# Main theorem


There exists a finitely generated torsion-free discrete group G together
with an injective homomorphism iotacolonZ^2↪ G and a
class cin H^2(BG;Z) such that, writing i=(Biota)colonmathbb T^2bbT^2to BG
for the corresponding map of classifying spaces and betain K_0(Cr(Z^2))
for the rank-zero Bott generator,
[
  ip{i^*c}{[mathbb T^2]}bbT^2]=1,
  
  iota_*(beta)=0quadin K_0(Cr(G)).
]
Consequently the class h=i_*[D_{mathbb T^2}bbT^2]in K_0(BG) has infinite
order, while its reduced assembly image mu_G^r(h) vanishes.


The two assertions are independent. The cohomology class c extends the
orientation class of the embedded Heisenberg torus and detects its Dirac
class through an index pairing; finite order of h is excluded by the
integer invariant. The vanishing iota_*(beta)=0 is proved by an
explicit operator-algebra construction in Cr(G), unaffected by that
invariant. Finite generation is a conclusion, not an input; finite
presentation is neither required nor claimed.

# Proof outline

The proof of Theorem~thm:main has three independent layers, combined
in Section~sec:assembly-v2.

(A) Deterministic labeled tower.: 
Sections~sec:labeling-v2 and~sec:pruning-v2 construct a
layered graph X_{old} with a folded labeling by a finite
alphabet and a deletion of a small fraction of every layer, such that:
(a) every immersed word of length at least 1.002m_i based at a vertex of
height i has at most one occurrence anywhere in the graph; (b) the
girth at height i exceeds .93m_i; (c) every bicycle of length <1.52m_i
has been removed. The parameter m_i is a linear function of a height
parameter H_i. A vertex state consists of two bit streams (u,v) in an
interleaved block layout; pairs of incident labels recover successive blocks
of a state via a two-evaluation step over a finite field. Random permutation
tables make the resulting incidences expanding, with a fixed spectral gap.

(B) Probabilistic voltages and symbols.: 
Section~sec:expansion-v2 chooses the permutation tables by a
sequential Lov'asz local lemma along the tower, preserving the expansion
gap while bounding the change of law at each update. Section~sec:analysis-v2
assigns to every surviving old edge a Heisenberg voltage h_einHH and a
bounded matrix weight W_ein M_m(C) so that the weighted incidence
approximates a prescribed compact Bott symbol in full regular-norm and so
that every short nonlaw of HH has, with overwhelming probability,
nontrivial holonomy.

(C) Operator-algebra cancellation and finite witness.: 
Sections~sec:geometry-v2--sec:assembly-v2 realize the
voltage cover inside the Cayley graph of a finitely generated group
Gamma, construct disjoint sheet domains so that the remaining
Cayley edges form a forest, and use the cone complex to prove
torsion-freeness and extend an integral degree-two detector from the
Heisenberg subgroup HH. The two explicit Eilenberg-swindle motions
of localized Bott profiles, followed by finite additivity, cancel the
initial-height profile in a norm-sequence quotient over a compatible
accuracy sequence. A finite K-theory witness then survives into a
single reduced group algebra Cr(G) for a finitely generated subgroup
GleGamma.

## Sequential probability law

Order each family's source blocks as A_0,B_1,A_1,B_2,A_2,...; interleave families by increasing source index and a fixed family order. At an unmarked update choose all active permutation variables uniformly. At a marked update use the finite conditional distribution given avoidance of current cut failures, separately in extra-bit slices.

[Sequential selection]
There is a probability law on the full tower such that every auxiliary incidence has centered singular norm at most rho:=1-.05^2/2<1 from Lemma~lem:mean-v2. At each source-block update, conditional on all earlier updates, any joint test of k specified current tables has probability bounded by e^{ε_s k} times its value under independent uniform tables. Proof. [Sketch]

# Pruning coherent returns and bicycles

Work with the sequential law of Proposition~prop:sequential-v2. The coefficient pools are already fixed. Let X^{good} be the graph induced on vertices surviving the gate and separation deletions of Lemmas~lem:gate-v2 and~lem:sep-v2; these are deterministic for every table realization. A statement that a path lies in X^{good} is a condition on the counted object, not a conditioning of the law. Lengths count edges. A bicycle is a finite connected subgraph with first Betti number two and no valence-one vertex.

## Aligned depths and coherent returns

For an A-position, put delta_A(j)=(2j+1)s; depth delta_A(j) means both raw A-streams agree through length r+js. For a B-position put delta_B(j)=2js; depth delta_B(j) means both raw B-streams agree through length js. These give the exact identities
delta_A(j)-delta_B(j)=s and delta_B(j)-delta_A(j-1)=s.

Let D(v) be the largest such depth with both prefixes active at v. By the terminal shapes of Lemma~lem:recovery-v2(i), H_i-2sle D(v)le H_i at height i. Two equal state prefixes propagate through one common outward edge as A_jmapsto B_j and B_jmapsto A_{j-1}, each losing exactly s of aligned depth.

[Coherent return]
In band k, a coherent return is a positive-length nonbacktracking path v_0,e_1,...,e_p,v_p in X^{good}, entirely in that band, whose endpoints lie on the same side and agree through an available aligned depth
[

## From bicycle pruning to Heisenberg nonlaws

Let HH=< x,y,zmid[x,y]=z,[x,z]=[y,z]=1>. Orientation the edges of a graph, associate a distinct variable t_e to each unoriented edge, extend t_{barbar e}e=t_e^{-1}. A path's word is an HH-law if it evaluates to the identity for every independent assignment t_einHH.

Hence ell(W)ge 4ell(B); in particular if every bicycle in the support has length ge T, then ell(W)ge 4T.


Proof. [Sketch]

For a paired e, write cyclically W=t_e^{ε}U t_e^{-ε}V and assign t_e=x, t_f=y, others identity. Then x^{ε}y^{n_f(U)}x^{-ε}y^{-n_f(U)}=z^{ε n_f(U)}, so n_f(U)=0; the interval between the two occurrences of any paired edge is balanced on every edge. Such intervals cannot cross.

If paired edges exist, choose an inclusion-minimal paired interval U (now heavy on every edge); if none, take U=W. In either case U is a nonempty reduced closed path, balanced, supported on heavy edges. Write U=PCP^{-1} with C cyclically reduced, still balanced, nonempty (else an adjacent reversal in the reduced U).

Delete valence-one vertices. ## Scope of the conclusion

(a) The theorem disproves reduced rational injectivity. It does not touch
maximal assembly: the Hanke--Schick low-degree nonvanishing result gives
mu_G^{max}(h) ⊗ 1ne0, and the regular quotient
lambda_Gcolon C^*_{max}(G)toCr(G) satisfies
mu_G^r=(lambda_G)_*mu_G^{max} by the completion comparison. The
class h is precisely a kernel class of the regular quotient.

(b) The classical Novikov conjecture asserts oriented homotopy invariance
of higher signatures; Theorem~thm:main does not falsify it. Higher
signatures already having homotopy invariance in low degree are subject
to the same Hanke--Schick result.

(c) The group G is finitely generated, torsion-free, and contains the
Heisenberg subgroup; it is not claimed to be finitely presented. This is
compatible with the Buss--Echterhoff--Willett erratum and with the
corrected Antonini--Buss--Engel--Siebenand statement, which assert only a
finitely presented version for the minimal exact crossed product.

(d) No claim of exactness of G (or of C^*_r(G)) is made.
