Consider a random planar curve whose dimension is strictly between one
and two. Ordinary arc length is useless: such a curve has infinite
length. One needs a finer ruler. For a positive number s and a
countable cover of a set E by blobs of diameters delta_1,delta_2,
..., form the cost sum_j delta_j^s, take the smallest cost over
covers with all diameters at most delta, and let delta↓0.
The limit is the s-dimensional Hausdorff measure H^s(E).
A dimension theorem pins down the critical power of the diameter, but at
exactly that power the measure may still be 0 or ∞. The task
is to find the right small correction to the power, called the gauge.

Schramm introduced stochastic Loewner evolution as the family of
candidate scaling limits obtained by driving the Loewner equation with
Brownian motion, and Lawler, Schramm and Werner proved that chordal
SLE_kappa arises as the scaling limit of loop-erased random
walk and of the contour curve of the uniform spanning tree.
./pdf/schramm-2000-scaling-limits-lerw-ust.pdf
./pdf/lawler-schramm-werner-2004-conformal-invariance-lerw-ust.pdf

For chordal Schramm-Loewner evolution SLE_kappa with
0<kappa<8, Beffara identified the dimension d=1+kappa/8.
./pdf/beffara-2008-dimension-of-sle-curves.pdf
Rezaei then showed the d-dimensional Hausdorff measure of the trace
is zero, so the critical power alone gives nothing and a correction is

The proof of the main theorem splits into two Hausdorff bounds. The

The remainder is organized as a chain of lemmas. Each is proved in
full; none borrows a proof from elsewhere. The first block is boundary
geometry of a rough past, which supplies the individual passages used
later. All closures at infinity in it are spherical. A filled hull
generated by a continuous curve means the curve together with the
components it disconnects from the target; the surviving domain is the
component toward the target. The continuous-trace description of
chordal Loewner evolution is used throughout, and the claims below
concern boundary geometry, not the existence of the trace. For the
fixed parameter 0<kappa<8 the continuous generating trace is that of

composition of the Loewner maps and the Brownian strong Markov

prefixes converge in the kernel sense as s varies, so their

uniformly, and reflection near infinity outside a ball containing the
slit gives convergence of the Laurent coefficients.

uniformly locally arcwise connected: on the simple compact arc, uniform

points of the arc close to the real line lie on a uniformly short
initial part, which can be joined to the half-plane through 0. Put
the inverse maps on the disk with a fixed coordinate; their spherical
