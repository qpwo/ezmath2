
A smooth complex projective variety X satisfies the ordinary Hodge
conjecture in degree 2r when every rational Hodge class in
H^{2r}(X) is a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic
cycles of codimension r. A stronger statement, geometric Hodge
conjecture, fixes for every d ge 0 and every rational sub-Hodge
structure H subset H^{2d}(X) of Hodge coniveau at least p a
closed algebraic subset Z subset X of codimension p supporting
H, that is H subset N^p H^{2d}(X,Q). For a projective K3
surface both conjectures are void: the cohomology has weight zero,
two, and four, and the classes of type (1,1) in H^2 are
exhausted by the Neron-Severi part via the Lefschetz (1,1)
theorem. Every question of substance concerns a self-power S^m.

The Kunneth formula produces three distinct sources of Hodge classes
on S^m: divisor classes pulled back from the factors, Tate classes
from the polarization and the fundamental class, and classes in the
tensor algebra of the transcendental lattice Tsubset H^2(S,Q).
The third source is the hard one. Kuga and Satake attach a polarized
rational Hodge structure of weight one to any quadratic space of K3
type by forming its even Clifford algebra.

./pdf/kuga-satake-1967-mathann-169.pdf

For a polarized K3 Hodge structure (T,q) of rank t and signature
(2,t-2), let W=C^+(T,q) be the even Clifford algebra, of
dimension 2^{t-1}. The complex structure on W is left
multiplication by J=e_1e_2 for any oriented orthonormal pair
e_1,e_2 in the positive period plane. Any nonisotropic w in T
defines a bilinear map

 j_w(v)(a) = v,a,w,  j_w : T(1) to End(W), 

and any trace polarization E of W converts it into

 iota_{w,E} : T(-1) to W  ⊗  W. 

The map j_w is a morphism of Hodge structures for the left spin
action, so iota_{w,E} is absolutely Hodge; it depends only on
(T,q) and E, not on any marking. Van Geemen's survey records the algebraic
and Hodge-theoretic side, including the half-spin decomposition
used below; Deligne developed the construction in families to
reach the Weil conjectures for K3 surfaces.

./pdf/vangeemen-1999-math-9903146-kuga-satake.pdf

The first main assertion is a bookkeeping claim: algebraicity of one
exact Kuga-Satake tensor at a generic point of a family forces
algebraicity of the whole tensor algebra. Exterior powers, Clifford
multiplication, algebraic adjoints, and Cayley-Hamilton inverses are
all rational algebraic constructions; the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem
closes the final gap. Ordinary HC follows for every self-power of the
source K3, and geometric coniveau support follows for every rational
sub-Hodge structure. The endomorphism-field cases, CM and totally
real, require separate handling of the Galois-eigenspace
projectors, but the reasoning is uniform.

The second main assertion, and the geometric heart of the
construction, is a specific K3 family in which the required cycle
exists. Let P be a primitive rank-eight sublattice of the K3
lattice with signature (2,6), discriminant 16, and containing an
ample class. Fix a Clifford model W=C^+(U_P ⊗  Q,q) and a
nonisotropic w in U_P  ⊗  Q. For every projective K3 surface
S whose Neron-Severi lattice contains P primitively, the tensor
iota_{w,E} is algebraic: a cycle in CH^2(Stimes A^2)_Q
realizes it on the whole of U_P  ⊗  Q. The moduli space of such
surfaces has complex dimension six; its complex points form the K3
quadratic locus of P.

Combining the two assertions gives HC and GHC for every self-power
of every K3 surface in the quadratic locus. The construction is
unconditional: no standard conjecture, no moment map, and no special
deformation is used. Six steps deliver the proof.

(i) A general section of O_B(2H) on an abelian surface B,
together with a nontrivial two-torsion character chi, produces a
double cover X of B. Resolving the inversion quotient gives a
surface Y carrying a rank-eight variation U with form U_P,
identified with the anti-invariant part of H^2(X) under the
involution.

(ii) A Gauss-pencil argument produces two curves C_1,C_2 and a
morphism C_1times C_2 to X that is generically finite of total
degree eight. When U_X carries no nonzero rational (1,1) class,
an algebraic correspondence injects U_X into
H^1(C_1,Q) ⊗  H^1(C_2,Q).

(iii) The variation U is realized in an auxiliary K3 family S^0
over the original pencil line, isometrically and as a Hodge
substructure of R^2(S^0/O)_*Q.

(iv) Two successive square substitutions t=s+u^2 and s=b+v^2
convert the surface comparison into a degree-three comparison
between rational threefolds, splitting by curve-cohomology and
cylinder classes.

(v) The degree-three comparison recovers the exact tensor j_w at a
construction point. A universal cycle then transports the fixed
algebraic source to every target of the quadratic locus by a
Hilbert-scheme and Baire argument.

(vi) A rational-polarization transfer step, applying the compression
identity that projects the full even Clifford module onto a
submodule at a Picard jump, extends the result to every prescribed
polarization and every rational Hodge identification.


Fix a projective K3 surface S with transcendental lattice (T,q) of
rank t. Put V=T(1) and W=C^+(T,q)=H^1(A,Q), the even Clifford
algebra realized as the first cohomology of a Kuga-Satake abelian
variety A. Write E for a rational polarization of W and
 eta_W : V to W ⊗  W(-1) 
for the tensor form of the bilinear map v mapsto vaw built from a
fixed nonisotropic win T, after absorbing E in the target. When
the dependence on w and E matters, the notation is eta_{w,E};
usually w and E are fixed and w is dropped.

Throughout, cycles have rational coefficients, cohomology is rational
Betti cohomology, and integral lattices are taken modulo torsion. A
cycle Z in CH^r(Xtimes Y)_Q acts on cohomology by
 Z_* : H^k(X) to H^{k+2r-2dim X}(Y), 
   Z_*x = (p_Y)_*(cl(Z)smile p_X^*x), 
in untwisted notation. Degree-changing maps carry the corresponding
Tate twist as Hodge maps. Only cohomological projectors that are
represented by cycles are used; idempotence is never required modulo
rational equivalence.

The first engine is a general book-keeping statement. Assume the
single tensor eta_W is algebraic, that is, assume that a cycle
Gamma in CH^2(Stimes A^2)_Q acts on T through the
map j_w(v)(a)=vaw. Then the entire exterior tensor algebra of T is
algebraic, with explicit codimension bookkeeping.

Proposition. Suppose the Kuga-Satake tensor eta_W=j_w is algebraic.
Fix 1 le k le t and put
Z=A^2, n=dim Z. Then there exist correspondences
 Gamma_k in CH^{k+1}(S^ktimes Z)_Q, 
   R_k in CH^{n+k-1}(Ztimes S^k)_Q, 
such that (Gamma_k)_*|_{wedge^k T} = F_k and (R_k)_*F_k=id,
where F_k : wedge^k T to B_W(1-k) is the k-th exterior power of
j_w composed with the Clifford product. Moreover, every rational
Hodge class in (wedge^k V)^{0,0}_Q is the class of a rational
algebraic cycle of codimension k on S^k.

Proof. Represent eta_W by the cycle Gamma in the hypothesis,
first projecting both slots by pi_T on the S-side and by
pi_1^Aboxtimespi_1^A on the A^2-side. The k-fold exterior
product of this cycle, followed by the antisymmetrizing projector
pi_{wedge,k} = (1)/(k!)sum_{sigma}pmsigma, produces a
correspondence
 Gamma_k^0 in CH^{k+1}(S^ktimes Z)_Q, 
because each factor contributes codimension 1 in S^k and 2 in
Z=A^2. On a Kunneth monomial x_1 ⊗ ... ⊗  x_k with
each x_iin T, the action of Gamma_k^0 before Clifford pairing
is sum_sigma pm j_w(x_{sigma(1)})... j_w(x_{sigma(k)})
inside End(W), i.e. the alternating product wedge^k j_w.
Composition in End(W) contracts (k-1) pairs of W-slots
with the polarization e in W ⊗  W, and each such contraction is
algebraic: the class
 (Delta_A)_*(ell^{g-1}) in CH^{2g-1}(A^2)_Q 
regarded as a correspondence to a point realizes W ⊗  W to Q(-1)
and lowers cohomological degree by two, with g=dim A and ell the
polarization underlying E. The two uncontracted W-slots make
up the surviving Z=A^2 factor, and any Koszul sign from permuting
odd-degree factors is absorbed into the rational coefficients. The
resulting cycle is Gamma_k in CH^{k+1}(S^ktimes Z)_Q,
and its degree shift 2-2k matches
2((k+1)-dim S^k), with output twist 1-k.

For the left inverse, put L=pr_1^*ell + pr_2^*ell
on Z. The Clifford summand B_W of H^2(Z,Q) is primitive in
degree two for L: expanding L^{n-1} in a Kunneth basis, any term
that survives on B_W has odd total degree on each abelian factor,
whereas the only top bidegree available on A^2 is (2g,2g). Hence
L^{n-1}B_W=0. The Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations show that
 Q_Z(x,y) = int_{Z} xsmile ysmile L^{n-2} 
is a polarization on B_W up to a fixed sign. Retwisting the
untwisted F_k gives a morphism of weight-two Hodge structures from
(wedge^k T)(k-1) to B_W. The image is therefore a Hodge
substructure of B_W, and the restriction of Q_Z to this
substructure is nondegenerate: an associated definite Hermitian form
restricts to a definite form on a complex subspace.

Multiplication by L^{n-2}, followed by the transpose {}^tGamma_k
of Gamma_k and the antisymmetrizer pi_{wedge,k}, defines an
algebraic map
 G_k : H^2(Z,Q) to wedge^k T, 
   G_k in CH^{n+k-1}(Ztimes S^k)_Q, 
whose cohomological degrees are
H^2(Z) xrightarrow{L^{n-2}} H^{2n-2}(Z) xrightarrow{{}^tGamma_k} H^{2k}(S^k).
If Q_k denotes middle intersection on wedge^k T, self-adjointness
of pi_{wedge,k} gives
 Q_k(G_k F_k x, y) = Q_Z(F_k x, F_k y). 
The right side is a polarization and is therefore nondegenerate; the
left side is Q_k(D_k x, y) with D_k = G_k F_k. Hence D_k is an
automorphism of the finite-dimensional space wedge^k T.

Its characteristic polynomial has nonzero constant term, and
Cayley-Hamilton supplies P_kin Q[X] with P_k(D_k)D_k = id
on wedge^k T. Composition of cycles realizes P_k(D_k) in
codimension 2k on S^ktimes S^k, using the projector pi_{wedge,k}
as the identity of the summand. Thus
 R_k = P_k(D_k),G_k in CH^{n+k-1}(Ztimes S^k)_Q,
    R_k F_k = id, 
and R_k maps H^2(Z,Q)(1) to (wedge^k T)(k) in weight-zero
notation. The construction uses multiplication by an ample class,
transposition, and a polynomial inverse on a finite-dimensional
summand; it needs no algebraic inverse to a Lefschetz operator.

Now let alpha in (wedge^k V)^{0,0}_Q be a rational Hodge class.
Its image F_kalpha is a rational (1,1) class inside B_W, so the
Lefschetz (1,1) theorem produces a divisor class on Z representing
F_k alpha up to a rational scalar. Applying R_k recovers
alpha, and the resulting cycle on S^k has codimension
1+(n+k-1)-n = k. The composite Gamma_k circ R_k, of codimension
n on Ztimes Z, is the corresponding cohomological projector onto
F_k(wedge^k T)subset H^2(Z,Q). That completes the proof.


The second engine turns the exterior returns of the exterior-returns proposition
into the ordinary Hodge conjecture on S^m and the geometric Hodge
conjecture on self-powers. Two inputs are needed: a coniveau statement
for cycles in a product, and a carrier bound for tensor Hodge classes
in terms of the endomorphism field.

Lemma (support of a correspondence). Let X,Y be smooth projective,
dim X=N, dim Y=D, and let CinCH^{N+p}(Xtimes Y)_Q
with pge 0. Then there is a fixed closed subset Bsubset Y of
codimension at least p with C_*H^j(X,Q)subset N^p H^{j+2p}(Y,Q).

Proof. Choose a finite cycle representative for C. Every component of
a representative supported in CH^{N+p}(Xtimes Y) has
dimension D-p, so its proper image in Y has dimension at most
D-p. The union B of the proper images has codimension at least
p. The restriction of the correspondence to Xtimes(Y∖ B)
is zero, and compatibility of the correspondence action with
restriction gives the stated inclusion. Rational linear combinations
of components are handled by the same reasoning.

Now let (T,q) be the transcendental lattice of a projective K3
surface with Hodge-simple T. Put E=End_{Hdg}(T),
a=dim_E T, and V=T(1). The field E is a division algebra over
Q and is either totally real or CM. Write E^+ for the maximal
totally real subfield. On the period domain the Hodge group of V
is Hg(V)=Res_{E/Q}SO(T,phi) in the real
case, where phi is a symmetric E-bilinear form whose trace is q,
and Hg(V)=Res_{E^+/Q}U(T,phi) in the CM
case. Zarhin's description of the Hodge group in these two forms is
restated, with the conventions used below, in Van Geemen's survey on
real multiplication.

./pdf/vangeemen-2006-math-0609839-real-mult.pdf

Fix an algebraic closure of Q inside C over which these groups
split. In the real case, after extending scalars, the eigenspace
decomposition reads
 V_K = ⊕_{eta : E↪ K} V_eta, 
   dim_K V_eta = a, 
with the summands mutually orthogonal and nondegenerate for q; the
Hodge group becomes the product of full special orthogonal groups
prod_eta SO(V_eta,q) after extending scalars further. In
the CM case, for each conjugate pair {eta,bareta} the form q
pairs V_eta perfectly with V_{bareta} and trivially with all
other summands, and the Hodge group becomes prod_eta GL(W_eta)
with V_eta simeq W_eta ⊕  W_eta^*, dim W_eta = a. The
distinguished embedding whose summand contains V^{1,-1} is chosen
representative of its conjugate pair, so on that factor the Hodge
cocharacter is conjugate to diag(z,1,...,1) and is
trivial elsewhere.

Lemma (carrier bound). Let Hsubset H^{2d}(S^m,Q) be a nontrivial
rational simple sub-Hodge structure of Hodge coniveau at least p, and
assume dge p. Then there exist 1le cle d-p and integers
1le k_1,...,k_cle t such that the exterior carrier
 C_k = ⊗_{i=1}^c wedge^{k_i}T subset H^{2s}(S^s,Q),
    s=sum_{i=1}^c k_i, 
admits a surjective rational Hodge map C_k(s) to H(d).

Proof. The Kunneth decomposition identifies H^{2d}(S^m,Q)(d) with a
direct sum of tensor powers of V, after discarding the
Tate-Q contributions of H^0, of the Neron-Severi part of H^2,
and of H^4. So H(d) is a rational simple representation of
G=Hg(V) of Hodge level at most 2(d-p).

Real case. Fix the complexified Hodge cocharacter so that its
eigenvalues on V_eta are 1,0,...,0,-1 in the distinguished
summand and 0 in all others. A complex irreducible constituent of
H_C has, on each factor V_eta with dim V_eta = a, highest
weight lambda_eta in the integral weights of SO_a, i.e.
 lambda_{eta,1}ge...gelambda_{eta,l}ge 0  (a=2l+1),
   
   lambda_{eta,1}ge...gelambda_{eta,l-1}ge |lambda_{eta,l}|
    (a=2l). 
Half-integral spin weights are not representations of tensor powers of
the standard module and do not occur. For the distinguished
embedding eta_0, the extremal eigenvalues of the Hodge cocharacter
on V_{eta_0} are lambda_{eta_0,1} and -lambda_{eta_0,l};
taking the maximum of the Weyl orbit of the highest weight displays
both. The Hodge level on that factor is therefore 2lambda_{eta_0,1}
when lambda_lge 0, and 2|lambda_{eta_0,l}| when that
distinguished irrep is genuinely a middle-weight one; in either case,
2max{lambda_{eta_0,1},|lambda_{eta_0,l}|} le 2(d-p), so
max{lambda_{eta_0,1},|lambda_{eta_0,l}|} le d-p. Define
c = max_eta max{lambda_{eta,1},|lambda_{eta,l}|}; then
cle d-p. For each eta and each column index jin{1,...,c}
fix the signed orthogonal columns of lambda_eta, with heights
h_{eta,j}, padding by zeros where j>lambda_{eta,1}; when a is
even and j is the middle column, both signs +|lambda_{eta,l}|
and -|lambda_{eta,l}| are permitted and both give the same
construction. Put k_j = sum_eta h_{eta,j}, so that
 ⊗_eta wedge^{h_{eta,j}}V_eta
    subset wedge^{k_j}V_C. 
Taking Cartan components in each factor and using the dominant-weight
theorem realizes the desired tuple of irreducibles inside
⊗_{j=1}^c wedge^{k_j}V_C. The number of exterior factors
c is at most d-p, as required.

CM case. For each conjugate pair {eta,bareta} choose
one representative eta. A complex irreducible constituent of H_C has, on
each factor W_eta, an integral highest weight
 lambda_{eta,1}ge...gelambda_{eta,a} 
for the full general linear group; negative entries and central
determinant characters are kept. Extremal eigenvalues of the
distinguished cocharacter on that factor are lambda_{eta,1} and
lambda_{eta,a}, again because the weight polytope is the convex
hull of the permutation orbit of the highest weight. Set
w_eta = max{lambda_{eta,1}, -lambda_{eta,a}, 0}. The Hodge
level bound of H(d) yields w_{eta_0}le d-p, and Galois
transports transporters of conjugate pairs to the distinguished
factor, so that rationality of H gives w_etale d-p for all
eta. Put c = max_eta w_eta. For each eta and
jin{1,...,c} fix a signed full-height column of heights
h_{eta,j}, and set k_j = sum_eta h_{eta,j}. Since the
exterior power of a direct sum includes
⊗_eta wedge^{h_{eta,j}}V_etasubsetwedge^{k_j}V_C,
taking Cartan components in each factor puts the chosen irreducible
inside ⊗_{j=1}^cwedge^{k_j}V_C, again with cle d-p.
The ranks three and four are the single-coordinate cases, and columns
of height one and two suffice.

Finally, rational descent. The multisets of conjugate constituents are
Galois-invariant because H is rational. Taking invariants in
(C_k)^* ⊗  H and scalar-extending an invariant tensor gives a
nonzero rational map C_k to H, and rational simplicity of H
makes any such map surjective. Because the Hodge cocharacter lies in
G, this map is also a Hodge map. That finishes the proof.

Apply the two lemmas. Let Hsubset H^{2d}(S^m,Q) be a rational
sub-Hodge structure of Hodge coniveau at least p, where
0le ple d. By semisimplicity H may be taken simple. If H has
type (d,d), ordinary HC and a finite basis of cycle classes put H
inside N^d H^{2d}(S^m,Q) subset N^p H^{2d}(S^m,Q). Otherwise the
carrier bound provides cle d-p, k_1,...,k_cge 1, and a
surjective rational Hodge map
 f : C_k(s) to H(d), 
   C_k = ⊗_{i=1}^cwedge^{k_i}T, 
   s=sum_i k_i. 
Extend f by zero on a rational Hodge complement of C_k(s) in
H^{2s}(S^s,Q)(s), which exists by polarizability. Poincare duality
identifies the extended map with a rational Hodge class of codimension

Now use the returns R_{k_i} from that proposition, with Z=A^2 and
n=dim Z. Taking the exterior product of the returns, with factors reordered gives

whose image contains C_k: the tensor product of the F_{k_i} is a

The two engines combine into the main engine statement: if in one K3
surface S the exact Kuga-Satake tensor eta_W:Vto W ⊗  W(-1)
is realized by a cycle on Stimes A^2, then ordinary HC and geometric
GHC hold for every self-power of S. Algebraicity of the exterior
tensor algebra and the rational carrier bound therefore give this
in full. The remaining task is a family of K3 surfaces in which
eta_W is visibly algebraic. That family is a K3 quadratic locus of
a primitive rank-eight lattice P of signature (2,6) and
discriminant 16.

A double cover of an abelian surface produces this family. Fix a
principally polarized abelian surface (B,H) and a nontrivial
two-torsion character chiin B[2]^vee. Put M=O_B(H),
and choose a smooth divisor Din|2H| avoiding B[2]. Ample
vanishing and Riemann-Roch on B give h^0(B,Mchi)=1,
h^0(B,M^2)=4, and vanishing of all higher cohomology for these
line bundles. The linear system |M^2| is basepoint-free, and the
normalized symmetric linearization of M^2 fixes every section.
Standard theta constructions identify the four second-order
theta sections with even theta functions theta[ε;0](2z,2tau)
for εin(frac12 Z/Z)^2, and the distinct Fourier congruence
classes give a basis.

./pdf/peters-sterk-2024-quadratic-forms.pdf

For each section [d]in P H^0(B,M^2), let D=(d=0). Bertini and
basepoint-freeness give a nonempty open in which D is smooth and
avoids B[2]: the finitely many evaluation hyperplanes at B[2] are
avoided. Every such divisor is invariant under inversion, since the
normalized section is symmetric. The resulting parameter space is an
open in a projective three-space bundle over the three-dimensional
abelian-data chart, and has dimension six.

Let pi:B'to B be the degree-two isogeny attached to chi^2, so
pi_*O_{B'}=O_B ⊕ chi^2. Trivialize
pi^*chi^2 and put L=pi^*(Mchi). The double cover
 X=Spec_{B'}(O_{B'} ⊕  L^{-1}) → B',
    z^2=pi^*d 
is a smooth surface. The chosen trivialization identifies L^2 with
pi^*M^2, and pi^*d defines the multiplication in this algebra.
Write delta for the sheet involution of Xto B' and j for
the involution induced by inversion of B. The two commute on X.

The quotient Y=X/j is singular along the image of
X^{delta j}; resolving it gives a smooth surface Y called the
resolved inversion quotient. The relevant local models are as
follows. Away from the branch curve, a positive-sign fixed point of
j on X has model (u,v)mapsto(-u,-v), whose quotient has two
ordinary double points; resolving the two singular points introduces two disjoint
(-2)-curves. At an exchanged pair away from the branch, the local
double surface is z^2=xy+t, whose vanishing sphere has square -2
and is negated by delta. At a negative-sign fixed point with
branch exceptional curve, j(x,y,z)=(-x,-y,-z) acts antipodally on
the real vanishing sphere, hence by -1 on its degree-two class, so
the j-invariant quotient receives no negative-eigenvalue class.
These local models build the smooth double cover Yto K of the
Kummer surface.

The sheet-invariant rational cohomology of Y is the pullback of
H^2(K,Q) with the cup pairing multiplied by two, and its signature
is (3,19). The perpendicular V_Qsubset H^2(Y,Q) has signature
(2,14) and is even: for vin V_Q the canonical class K_Y is
invariant, vcdot K_Y=0, and the parity relation
v^2equiv vcdot K_Ypmod 2 holds on the even unimodular lattice.
For the integral structure, unimodular gluing of the two primitive
eigensublattices of H^2(Y,Z)/tors shows that both
discriminant groups are two-elementary, and monodromy acts trivially
on the discriminant groups for fixed B'.

Denote by a_1,...,a_8 the eight sheet-anti-invariant classes
a_i=[E_i^+]-[E_i^-] coming from the positive-sign fixed points, with
E_i^pm the two exceptional curves exchanged by delta. Each has
a_i^2=-4, the eight are mutually orthogonal, and the span is a
flat sub-bundle of V_Q under parallel transport. Its perpendicular
Usubset V_Q is the sheet-anti-invariant Hodge summand of Y; it has
rank eight and signature (2,6) and is flat. On a common resolution

 (beta x,beta y)_X = 2(x,y)_Y 
for beta:Xto Y; in particular U is isometric to U_X over the

The transfer from the enlarged Hodge structure U_P to the
transcendental structure T_S of an arbitrary surface in the
quadratic locus proceeds by the compression identity.

Lemma (compression). Let i:W_T↪ W_U be an inclusion of
polarizable rational weight-one Hodge structures, let p:W_Uto W_T
be a Hodge retraction, and let E_U,E_T be rational polarizations.
Let H be a rational Hodge structure of weight zero. For a Hodge map
j:HtoEnd(W_U), write iota_{j,E_U}:H(-1)to W_U ⊗  W_U
for its tensor form. Put
 b = (E_T^flat)^{-1}i^vee(-1)E_U^flat : W_Uto W_T. 
Then
 (p ⊗  b),iota_{j,E_U} = iota_{pji,E_T}. 
If the weight-one structures are abelian H^1 spaces, both maps on
the tensor slots are induced by rational algebraic correspondences.

Proof. In the ordering End(W_U)=W_U ⊗  W_U^vee, tensor
conversion is id ⊗ (E_U^flat)^{-1} after the common
Tate twist. So the left side is
 (p ⊗ (E_T^flat)^{-1}i^vee(-1))j, 
which is exactly the tensor conversion of pji. All maps are rational
Hodge maps.

For the algebraic statement, let f:H^1(B,Q)to H^1(A,Q) be a rational
Hodge map between abelian varieties. Its dual on homology commutes with
complex structures, and for some positive integer n, nf^vee is
integral and induces a holomorphic homomorphism phi:Ato B.
Projectivity and GAGA make phi algebraic, and
f=phi^*/n. Therefore [Gamma_phi^t]/ninCH^{dim B}(Btimes A)_Q
induces f. The polarization dual E_T^flat and its inverse are
divisor classes on A_Utimes A_U; the inclusion i and its dual
i^vee arise from the product maps along the Clifford inclusion of
W_T into W_U, which is algebraic for the same reason, applied
componentwise.

Now the main transfer. Let S be an ample P-polarized K3 surface.
For every prescribed nonisotropic win T_S, rational polarization E,
and transported even-Clifford realization of C^+(T_S,q), the standard
tensor iota_{w,E} has a rational codimension-two algebraic
realization.

Proof. Put U=U_P and M=UcapNS(S)_Q. Since P contains an
ample class, the Hodge index theorem makes M negative definite, so
the orthogonal decomposition
 U = T_S perp M 
holds over Q. The projection onto the first summand is algebraic:
choose divisor bases of NS(S)_Q and use the inverse
intersection matrix together with the diagonal correspondence.

The specialization proposition gives an exact tensor
iota_{w_0,E_0} on the whole of U.

For the desired win T_S, put r=w_0^{-1}win C^+(U,q). It is
invertible; right multiplication R_r commutes with the left Hodge
complex structure, so it is a rational Hodge automorphism, and
associativity gives
 j_w = R_r j_{w_0}, 
   iota_{w,E_0} = (R_r ⊗ id)iota_{w_0,E_0}. 
This is a change in the first tensor slot; no other factors change.

The Clifford inclusion
i:W_T=C^+(T_S,q)↪ W_U=C^+(U,q) is injective by the
Poincare-Birkhoff-Witt theorem and is a Hodge map, since the left
period bivector lies in C^+(T_S,q) on both spaces. Polarizability
gives a rational Hodge retraction p. Apply the compression lemma
with the desired E on W_T.

For v,win T_S, j_w(v) preserves W_T and restricts there to the
standard operator amapsto vaw. Consequently
 (pR_r ⊗ (E^flat)^{-1}i^vee(-1)E_0^flat)
       iota_{w_0,E_0}
 = iota^{T_S}_{w,E}. 
The order matters: R_r itself need not preserve W_T when
w_0notin T_S, but the resulting operator j_w does.

Both tensor-slot maps are algebraic on abelian H^1 structures by the
compression lemma. After clearing denominators, the product
homomorphism on the two abelian factors and its normalized transpose
graph realize the tensor product, with no unrecorded permutation
sign. The normalized transpose graph has codimension
2dim A_U=dim A_U^2, so postcomposition preserves codimension two.
Precompose with the algebraic source projector and postcompose with
algebraic degree-one projectors on the abelian factors. The full
degree-two action is then exactly the displayed tensor. The same
argument with the prescribed rational Hodge identification transports
it to any stipulated isogenous model.

Theorem. Let P be the primitive rank-eight sublattice of the K3
lattice with signature (2,6) and discriminant 16. Let S be a
projective K3 surface with P primitive in NS(S). Then for
every rational polarization E and every nonisotropic win T_S, the
standard Kuga-Satake tensor
 iota_{w,E} : T_S(-1) to W_T ⊗  W_T 
is algebraic: a cycle in CH^2(Stimes A^2)_Q realizes it on
the whole of U_P, hence in particular on T_S. Consequently ordinary
HC and geometric GHC hold for every self-power of S.

Proof. The algebraic realization applies at every rational
polarization and every nonisotropic w by the transfer proposition.
Exterior power, algebraic adjoints, and Cayley-Hamilton give the
exterior-returns proposition at every k. The geometric coniveau
argument then gives ordinary HC for every self-power of S and
geometric GHC for every rational sub-Hodge structure of
H^{2d}(S^m,Q).

Corollary (lattice-isometric partners). Every projective K3 surface
whose transcendental lattice is isometric to (U_P,q) satisfies
ordinary HC and geometric GHC for every self-power.

Corollary (Fourier-Mukai partners). Let S' be a projective K3
surface derived-equivalent to a P-polarized K3 S in the quadratic
locus. Then S' satisfies ordinary HC and geometric GHC for every
self-power of S'.

Proof. A Fourier-Mukai transform gives an algebraic correspondence
between S and S' that is an isomorphism on the rational full
cohomology. Transport of algebraic cycles along this correspondence
preserves HC and GHC on products. The moduli space of Fourier-Mukai
partners of a given K3 is finite and the transcendental lattices of the partners are
isometric.

Corollary (moduli of stable sheaves). Let S be a P-polarized K3
surface with P primitive and vinwidetilde H(S,Z) a primitive Mukai vector
of positive rank. Let M=M_v(S) be the smooth projective moduli space
of Gieseker-stable sheaves (or of Bridgeland-stable objects in
D^b(S)). Then M^r satisfies ordinary HC and geometric GHC for
every rge1. If M is hyperkaehler, the same conclusions hold for
every smooth projective hyperkaehler variety birational to M and
all of its self-powers.

Proof. B"ulles's theorem on the motive of M_v(S) expresses h(M_v(S))
as a direct summand of a finite sum of Tate twists of motives of
powers h(S^k); the summands corresponding to symmetric products are
cut out by rational averaging projectors. Tensoring gives an analogous
splitting of every self-power h(M^r), again through Tate twists of
powers of S. In the language of Arapura, M is motivated by S and
therefore inherits ordinary HC and geometric GHC from S. The
hyperkaehler and birational statements use Riess's specialization
correspondence for birational smooth projective hyperkaehler
varieties, giving an isomorphism of Chow motives as formulated in
B"ulles.
