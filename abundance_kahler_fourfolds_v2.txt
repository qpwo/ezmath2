============================================================
ABUNDANCE AFTER NONVANISHING FOR COMPACT KAHLER FOURFOLDS

============================================================



============================================================
PART II. THE MAIN THEOREM AND EVERY IMPORTANT WORD
============================================================

5. The main theorem
-------------------
Theorem (Abundance after nonvanishing).
Let X be a normal connected compact Kähler complex space of dimension
four.  Let Delta be an effective rational Weil divisor such that
(X,Delta) is Kawamata log terminal and the actual adjoint
D = K_X + Delta is Q-Cartier.  Assume:

  (i) D is analytically nef;
  (ii) kappa(X,D) >= 0, i.e. some positive Cartier multiple of D has
       a nonzero holomorphic section.

Then there is an integer m > 0 for which mD is Cartier and the
evaluation map

    H^0(X, O_X(mD)) tensor_C O_X  -->  O_X(mD)

is surjective at every point.

If kappa(X,D) = 0, one may choose m so that

    O_X(mD) is isomorphic to O_X.

6. What "actual Q-Cartier adjoint" means
----------------------------------------
A naive definition of K_X + Delta requires K_X and Delta separately to
be Q-Cartier.  In many analytic problems they are not.  The paper uses
a sheaf-theoretic convention.

Let omega_X be the canonical sheaf, and let omega_X^[r] be its
reflexive r-th power.  For a rational divisor Delta, choose an integer
r > 0 clearing denominators and define

    L_r = ( omega_X^[r] tensor O_X(r Delta) )^{**}.

We say D = K_X + Delta is an actual Q-Cartier adjoint if L_r is an
invertible holomorphic line bundle for some r.  Then positive multiples
of D are tensor powers of L_r, after dividing the exponent by r.  This
avoids choosing a global canonical divisor and avoids pretending that
K_X or Delta is separately Q-Cartier.

All statements about sections, nefness, and semiampleness refer to this
actual holomorphic line bundle.

7. What "analytically nef" means
--------------------------------
Fix a Kähler form omega on X.  A rational line D is analytically nef
if for every epsilon > 0, a fixed Cartier multiple of D has a smooth
Hermitian metric h such that its normalized curvature satisfies

    Theta_h >= - epsilon omega

in the sense of local smooth potentials on X.  This is the natural
analytic replacement for "intersects every curve nonnegatively" when X
is not projective and curves may not sweep out enough directions.

8. What "klt" and "dlt" mean
----------------------------
Klt means Kawamata log terminal.  On a log resolution, the crepant
boundary has all coefficients strictly below 1.  Intuitively, the
singularities are milder than ordinary log canonical singularities.

Dlt means divisorial log terminal.  It is a standard weakening that
allows coefficient-one components in a controlled simple-normal-crossing
way.  The reduced floor S = floor(B) is the union of coefficient-one
components.  Dlt pairs are klt away from that floor.

9. What "semiample" means
-------------------------
A rational line D is semiample if some positive Cartier multiple mD is
generated by global sections.  That means for every point x there is a
section s of mD with s(x) != 0.  Equivalently, the evaluation map

    H^0(X, O_X(mD)) tensor O_X --> O_X(mD)

is surjective.  Then the sections define a holomorphic map to projective
space.

10. What "Iitaka dimension" means
---------------------------------
Kappa(X,D) measures how many independent sections positive multiples of
D have and how large their images are.  If every positive multiple has
no nonzero section, kappa = -infinity.  Otherwise kappa is the maximum
dimension of the image of the rational map defined by a complete linear
system of a positive multiple.

In this paper we only need two facts:

  * kappa >= 0 means some positive multiple has a nonzero section.
  * kappa = 0 means no positive multiple has two independent sections.
    If X is irreducible, two independent sections of one line would
    have a nonconstant meromorphic quotient, giving kappa >= 1.

============================================================
PART III. THE PROOF AT A GLANCE
============================================================

11. Step 1: The positive-Iitaka case
------------------------------------
Assume kappa(X,D) >= 1.  Das-Hacon-Paun's dlt modification theorem gives
a projective bimeromorphic map

    g : (X', Delta') --> (X, Delta)

with X' compact Kähler and ordinary Q-factorial, the pair dlt, and the
adjoint crepant.  Crepancy is an actual line identity:

    O_X'(m(K_X' + Delta')) is isomorphic to g^* O_X(mD)

for a common index m.  The pulled-back line is analytically nef, and its
sections are exactly the sections of mD on X.

Now apply the Horing-Lazic-Lehn theorem: a nef adjoint of positive
Iitaka dimension on an ordinary Q-factorial compact Kähler klt pair is
semiample, unconditionally through dimension four.  Thus the pulled-back
line on X' is semiample.  A generated multiple descends to X because
g_* O_X' = O_X.  So D is semiample on X.

Hence the only remaining case is kappa(X,D) = 0.

12. Step 2: The section and its divisor
---------------------------------------
Choose a nonzero section

    s in H^0(X, O_X(mD)).

Normalize its divisor:

    M = (1/m) div(s).

If M = 0, then s is nowhere zero.  A nowhere-zero section of a line
bundle is an isomorphism O_X -> O_X(mD).  So mD is trivial, and we are
done.

Assume for contradiction that M != 0.  We will build a supported model
and derive kappa >= 1, contradicting kappa = 0.

13. Step 3: The supported model
-------------------------------
Resolve X so that the support of Delta + M is simple normal crossing.
On the resolution, raise to coefficient one every exceptional prime and
every strict transform of a prime appearing in M.  This produces an
effective rational SNC boundary B_Y and a divisor P_Y with

    K_Y + B_Y ~_Q P_Y,
    Supp P_Y = Supp floor(B_Y),
    kappa(Y, K_Y + B_Y) = 0.

Then run the supported minimal model program for fourfolds with Kähler
targets.  The paper's technical work supplies the needed contractions
on threefold floors and extends them to the ambient fourfold.  At the
end one obtains a normal ordinary Q-factorial compact Kähler dlt
fourfold (V,B) and a nonzero effective rational Q-Cartier divisor P
such that

    A = K_V + B is analytically nef,
    A ~_Q P,
    Supp P = Supp floor(B),
    kappa(V,A) = 0.

The model also has a special projective resolution pi : Vhat -> V with:

  * globally smooth distinct SNC strict boundary and exceptional
    components;
  * every exceptional crepant coefficient strictly below 1;
  * pi generically an isomorphism on the image of every irreducible
    component of an intersection of distinct strict floor components.

Why does P survive?  Suppose P = 0.  Then the pullback of M to a common
resolution is an effective nonzero exceptional divisor.  Its line is
the pullback of the original nef D, so it is relatively nef.  But an
effective relatively nef exceptional divisor on a normal compact Kähler
space must be zero.  Contradiction.  Thus P != 0.

14. Step 4: Floor generation
----------------------------
Let S = floor(B).  The first boundary theorem is:

    A|_S is semiample.

The proof is an induction over the strata of S.  A stratum is an
irreducible component of an intersection of distinct floor components.
On each stratum Z, adjunction gives a normal compact Kähler dlt pair
(Z,B_Z) and an actual line identity

    O_Z(m(K_Z + B_Z)) is isomorphic to O_V(mA)|_Z.

The induction builds compatible sections on all strata up to dimension
d.  There are two cases when extending from the floor C_Z of a stratum Z
to Z itself.

Case 1: C_Z is vertical over the semiample target Y_Z of Z.
Then a torsion-free obstruction shows that every section of a large
multiple on C_Z extends automatically to Z.  No comparison is needed.

Case 2: C_Z is horizontal.
Then a Mori model reduces the problem to matching two marked points on
a general P^1 fiber.  The two markings come from the two branches of the
horizontal floor.  In even degree q, the residues of the logarithmic
form are

    a(w) xi^{tensor q}   and   (-1)^q a(w) xi^{tensor q},

which are equal because q is even.  This birational residue comparison
is called a link.  A section extends if its two pullbacks agree under
the link.

Links generate a groupoid on the surface strata.  To impose invariance,
we need finite isotropy actions on each fixed section space.  On
projective surfaces this is Fujino-Gongyo's log pluricanonical
representation theorem.  On nonprojective surfaces the paper uses the
single ambient Kähler class: a returning link preserves the class of the
ambient Kähler form modulo the Neron-Severi group.  The Hodge index
theorem forces the induced scalar on the holomorphic 2-form to be a
root of unity, and only finitely many orders are possible.  Thus the
scalar image is finite.

Now use norm products.  If G_Z is the finite isotropy image on
H^0(Z,L_Z^k), form

    P_Z(s_Z) = product_{g in G_Z} g(s_Z).

Choose a common h divisible by all |G_Z| and use P_Z(s_Z)^{h/|G_Z|}.
This raises the lower restrictions to a common power and makes the
section invariant.  Finite hyperplane avoidance then chooses enough
such invariant sections to generate at every point.  They agree on all
intersections by construction.  Proposition "strata adjunction" descends
them uniquely to the reduced floor S.  Therefore A|_S is semiample.

15. Step 5: Supported lifting
-----------------------------
The second boundary theorem is:

    If (V,B) is a normal irreducible compact Kähler dlt pair, A=K_V+B
    is the actual adjoint, and a nonzero effective rational Q-Cartier
    divisor P satisfies P ~_Q A and Supp P = Supp floor(B), then
    A|_S semiample implies kappa(V,A) >= 1.

Here is the idea.

Choose a sufficiently divisible even q and a line

    L = O_V(qA)

with a section s whose divisor is G = qP.  The semiampleness of A|_S
gives a morphism

    f : S -> T = P^b

and an actual identity L|_S = f^* O_T(1).

Near a compact fiber of f, take an r-th root of the defining section.
This produces a finite cover pi : Z -> W and a reduced Cartier divisor
E = (y=0) with

    O_Z(E) = pi^* P_1.

Let I = O_Z(-E).  The layers are A_j = I^j/I^{j+1}.  We need to lift
sections from E to all finite neighborhoods:

    For every j and k >= 1,
    g_* (I^j / I^{j+k+1})  -->  g_* (I^j / I^{j+k})
    is surjective.

The obstruction to lifting one order is the connecting map

    delta_k : g_* A_j --> R^1 g_* A_{j+k}.

It is a graded derivation.  If all delta_k vanish, every finite order
lifts.

To kill delta_k, take a second full normalized cover, this time using a
q-th root of s as a meromorphic q-pluricanonical form.  Resolve the
resulting cover and principalize the pulled ideal of E equivariantly.
Write H for the reduced SNC divisor above E.  The residue insertion
gives a split injection

    tau_* : R^j g_* A_{-a}  -->  R^j h_* omega_H.

Thus the obstruction sits inside a Hodge cohomology group R^1 h_* omega_H.

Filtered Hodge theory on the SNC support H gives a right D-module
M = H^1 p_+ K with

    F_0 M = R^1 h_* omega_H.

There is a first-symbol map

    sigma : F_0 M tensor T_T --> gr^F_1 M.

The key Hodge vanishing says: for every ample line N on a smooth
projective T,

    Hom_{O_T}(N, ker sigma) = 0.

Now fix a parameter point t_* in T.  Build a projective parameter cover
T' -> T which is unbranched over t_* and a single compact SNC graph over
the whole cover.  On this graph, the local adjugate identity turns the
obstruction into a global map

    R^m --> F_0 M' tensor T_{T'}.

Its image lies in ker sigma.  But R^m is ample.  The vanishing theorem
forces this map to be zero.  At the chosen point t_* the cover is
unbranched, so the obstruction itself vanishes there.  Since t_* was
arbitrary, delta_k = 0 everywhere.  Induction on k proves all finite
lifting orders.

Finally, use cyclic invariants.  Define

    J_j = O_V(-ceil(jG/r)),   j in Z.

These are coherent divisorial sheaves.  Their quotients B_j = J_j/J_{j+1}
are coherent O_S-modules.  Translation by G gives the periodicity

    F_{j-r} = F_j tensor O_T(1),

where F_j = f_* B_j.  For N > 0, the finite filtration of
F_{-Nr,0} has quotients F_j with -Nr <= j < 0.  Each such j is
uniquely j = l - tr with 0 <= l < r and 1 <= t <= N, so F_j = F_l(t).
Serre vanishing bounds the higher cohomology of all F_l(t) independently
of N.  At least one section of F_0(t) exists for every t >= 1, so the
zeroth cohomology grows at least linearly.  Therefore

    h^0(T, F_{-Nr,0}) >= N - C

for a constant C independent of N.  Lifting to V loses at most the fixed
finite dimension h^1(V,O_V).  Hence h^0(V, O_V(NG)) is unbounded.  For
some N, O_V(NG) has two independent sections.  Their quotient is a
nonconstant meromorphic function on V, so kappa(V,A) >= 1.

16. Step 6: Completion
----------------------
Return to the original X with kappa(X,D)=0 and M != 0.  The supported
model gives (V,B,P) as above.  Floor generation gives A|_S semiample.
Supported lifting then gives kappa(V,A) >= 1.  But the model was built
with kappa(V,A)=0.  Contradiction.

Therefore M = 0.  The original section s is nowhere zero, so

    O_X(mD) is isomorphic to O_X.

This proves the theorem in Iitaka dimension zero.  Together with the
positive-Iitaka case, the theorem is proved.

============================================================
PART IV. THE TECHNICAL ENGINE ROOM
============================================================

17. What the contraction machinery is for
-----------------------------------------
The fourfold program needs to contract negative extremal rays on
threefold floors and then extend those contractions to the ambient
fourfold.  The paper does not simply cite a black box for this.  It
builds the needed threefold contraction theorem from two analytic
constructions.

17.1 Finite null locus contraction.
Let X be a normal connected ordinary Q-factorial compact Kähler
threefold with klt singularities.  Let alpha be a nef and big real
(1,1)-class.  Suppose its null locus

    Null(alpha) = union of positive-dimensional V with alpha^{dim V}.V = 0

is a finite union of curves.  Then there is a proper bimeromorphic
morphism phi : X -> X' to a normal compact analytic space which is an
isomorphism away from C = Null(alpha), and whose nontrivial fibers are
exactly the connected components of C.

The proof resolves X, writes the pullback class as a Kähler class plus
an effective exceptional divisor, and uses Grauert's contraction
criterion for the conormal of C.  The key point is that a suitable
actual positive line on the resolution makes the conormal weakly
negative.

17.2 Conormal layers of a prime floor.
Let (X,S+B) be an ordinary plt pair, S an irreducible Q-Cartier prime,
dim S <= 3, and phi : S -> W a projective surjection with connected
fibers.  Suppose the actual lines -(K_S+B_S) and -S|_S are phi-ample.
Put I_k = O_X(-kS) and E_k = I_k/I_{k+1}.  Then each E_k is a rank-one
reflexive sheaf on S, and for a global Cartier index q there is an
effective rational divisor Xi_k on S with

    E_k^[q] = (O_X(-kqS)|_S) tensor O_S(-q Xi_k),
    0 <= Xi_k <= Theta <= B_S,

and

    R^i phi_* E_k = 0 for i > 0, k >= 1.

The sheaf Xi_k measures the defect between the k-th conormal layer and
the restriction of the actual line.  The vanishing is what makes the
analytic thickenings exist.

17.3 Analytic thickenings and prime-floor extension.
Using the conormal vanishings, the paper builds analytic thickenings

    S_k = V(I_k)

and proves that the pushforwards B_k = |phi|_* O_{S_k} are genuine
coherent analytic sheaves on W.  This lets one extend a contraction of
a prime floor S to the whole ambient space X.  The result is a proper
bimeromorphic morphism F : X -> Z with exactly the same fibers on S
and an isomorphism away from S.  This is Proposition "prime-floor
extension."

17.4 The threefold contraction theorem.
Combining finite-null contraction, prime-floor extension, the relative
projective MMP, and the specified positivity and rationality inputs
gives:

    Let (Z,Gamma) be a normal connected compact Kähler klt threefold
    with effective rational boundary and actual Q-Cartier adjoint
    A = K_Z + Gamma.  Suppose alpha = c_1(A) + [omega] is nef, where
    omega is Kähler.  Then there is a projective surjection f : Z -> Y
    with connected fibers, Y normal compact Kähler with rational
    singularities, and a Kähler class [omega_Y] such that
    alpha = f^*[omega_Y].  The line -A is f-ample.  A compact curve is
    contracted exactly when its alpha-degree is zero.

This theorem is used on floors of the fourfold program and on lower
strata in the boundary comparison.

18. What the Hodge-module theorem is for
----------------------------------------
The lifting obstruction lives in R^1 h_* omega_H on a simple
normal-crossing support H.  The paper proves two facts.

18.1 Filtered SNC direct image.
Let p : A -> T be a holomorphic map of complex manifolds, and let
i : H -> A be a reduced SNC divisor in a smooth complete intersection,
with h = p|_H proper.  Put

    K = H^c_[H](omega_A),
    F_0 K = image of Ext^c_A(O_H, omega_A) in K.

Then (K,F) is a good filtered regular holonomic right D-module.  For
M^j = H^j p_+ K with the induced filtration, every level cohomology map

    H^j F_p p_+(K,F) --> M^j

is injective with image F_p M^j.  The module has a finite filtration by
polarizable real pure Hodge modules.  In particular,

    F_0 M^j = R^j h_* omega_H,
    F_p M^j = 0 for p < 0.

This is the technical source of the residue injection and the lowest
piece R^1 h_* omega_H.

18.2 No ample line in the symbol kernel.
For the first-symbol map

    sigma : F_0 M^1 tensor T_T --> gr^F_1 M^1,

if T is smooth projective and N is ample, then

    Hom_{O_T}(N, ker sigma) = 0.

This is the vanishing that kills the global symbol map on the
projective parameter cover.  It is proved by comparing the real filtered
module with the Sabbah-Schnell pure Hodge module attached to the same
generic polarized variation, then applying negative-ample graded de
Rham vanishing.

19. What the special resolution is for
--------------------------------------
The final supported model (V,B) must have a projective log resolution
pi : Vhat -> V such that:

  * the strict boundary and exceptional support have globally smooth
    distinct SNC components;
  * every exceptional crepant coefficient is strictly below 1;
  * pi is generically an isomorphism on the image of every irreducible
    component of an intersection of distinct strict floor components.

This is Lemma "special resolution."  It is built from the normal common
graph of the finitely many MMP steps.  The key discrepancy calculation
is that every negative step strictly increases discrepancies over all
nontrivial fibers.  Hence a final log canonical center cannot be
swallowed by the bad locus.  The resolution is an isomorphism over the
open U_V where all steps are isomorphisms.  The complement of U_V has
codimension at least two, so the final exceptional support and floor
intersections are controlled.

20. Why the paper needs all this
--------------------------------
The fourfold theorem is not a formal consequence of threefold abundance.
The obstruction is genuinely four-dimensional: a boundary divisor can
wrap horizontally over the semiample target, and sections on its
components need not agree on intersections.  The paper solves this by:

  * proving the floor restriction is semiample;
  * proving that semiampleness of the floor forces positive Iitaka
    dimension when an effective supported representative exists;
  * doing both in the analytic compact Kähler category.

The technical engine room is the price of working without projectivity
or Q-factoriality.

PART V. FORMAL STATEMENTS AND DEPENDENCY CHART

21. Main theorem
----------------
Theorem.  Let X be a normal connected compact Kähler complex space of
dimension four.  Let Delta be an effective rational Weil divisor such
that (X,Delta) is klt and D = K_X + Delta is an actual Q-Cartier
adjoint.  If D is analytically nef and kappa(X,D) >= 0, then some
positive Cartier multiple mD is generated by global sections.  If
kappa(X,D)=0, then some positive multiple mD is isomorphic to O_X.

22. Supported model
-------------------
Proposition.  Under the hypotheses of the main theorem, suppose
kappa(X,D)=0 and the normalized divisor M of a nonzero plurisection of
D is nonzero.  Then there are a normal ordinary Q-factorial compact
Kähler dlt fourfold (V,B) with effective rational boundary and a
nonzero effective rational Q-Cartier divisor P such that

    A = K_V + B is analytically nef,
    A ~_Q P,
    Supp P = Supp floor(B),
    kappa(V,A) = 0.

The pair has the special projective resolution described above.

23. Floor generation
--------------------
Theorem.  Let (V,B) be a normal ordinary Q-factorial compact Kähler
dlt fourfold with effective rational boundary and analytically nef
actual adjoint A = K_V + B.  Suppose V has a projective log resolution
with globally smooth distinct SNC components, exceptional crepant
coefficients below 1, and generic isomorphism on every floor
intersection.  Then A|_S is semiample on the entire reduced floor
S = floor(B).

24. Supported lifting
---------------------
Theorem.  Let (V,B) be a normal irreducible compact Kähler dlt pair of
positive dimension, with effective rational boundary and actual
Q-Cartier adjoint A = K_V + B.  Suppose a nonzero effective rational
Q-Cartier divisor P satisfies

    P ~_Q A,   Supp P = Supp floor(B).

If A|_S is semiample on S = floor(B), then kappa(V,A) >= 1.

25. Threefold contraction
-------------------------
Proposition.  Let (Z,Gamma) be a normal connected compact Kähler klt
threefold with effective rational boundary and actual Q-Cartier adjoint
A = K_Z + Gamma.  Suppose

    alpha = c_1(A) + [omega]

is nef for some Kähler class [omega].  Then there is a projective
surjection f : Z -> Y with connected fibers, Y normal compact Kähler
with rational singularities, and a Kähler class [omega_Y] such that
alpha = f^*[omega_Y].  The line -A is f-ample.  A compact curve is
contracted exactly when its alpha-degree is zero.

26. Finite null locus contraction
---------------------------------
Proposition.  Let X be a normal connected ordinary Q-factorial compact
Kähler threefold with klt singularities.  Let alpha be a nef and big
real (1,1)-class whose null locus is a finite union of curves.  Then
there is a proper bimeromorphic morphism phi : X -> X' to a normal
compact analytic space such that phi is an isomorphism away from
Null(alpha), and the nontrivial fibers are exactly the connected
components of Null(alpha).

27. Conormal layers
-------------------
Proposition.  Let (X,S+B) be an ordinary plt pair with X a normal
connected compact Kähler space, S an irreducible effective Q-Cartier
prime, B >= 0 not containing S, and K_X + S + B an actual Q-Cartier
adjoint.  Suppose dim S <= 3 and phi : S -> W is a projective surjection
with connected fibers.  Suppose -(K_S+B_S) and -S|_S are phi-ample.
Put I_k = O_X(-kS) and E_k = I_k/I_{k+1}.  Then each E_k is a rank-one
reflexive sheaf on S.  For any positive global Cartier index q, there
is an effective rational divisor Xi_k on S such that

    E_k^[q] = (O_X(-kqS)|_S) tensor O_S(-q Xi_k),
    0 <= Xi_k <= Theta <= B_S,

and R^i phi_* E_k = 0 for i > 0, k >= 1.

28. Filtered SNC direct image
-----------------------------
Proposition.  For a reduced SNC support H in a smooth ambient A over T
as above, the filtered module K = H^c_[H](omega_A) is good, regular, and
holonomic.  The cohomology modules M^j = H^j p_+ K carry a finite
filtration by polarizable real pure Hodge modules.  Every level
cohomology map is injective, and

    F_0 M^j = R^j h_* omega_H,
    F_p M^j = 0 for p < 0.

29. No ample line in the symbol kernel
-------------------------------------
Corollary.  In the filtered SNC situation, if T is smooth projective
and N is ample, then

    Hom_{O_T}(N, ker sigma) = 0,

where sigma is the first-symbol map of M^1.

30. Dependency chart
--------------------
Here is the logical order of the proof.

    Main theorem
      |
      +-- kappa >= 1: Das-Hacon-Paun dlt modification
      |                + Horing-Lazic-Lehn abundance
      |
      +-- kappa = 0:
            |
            +-- Supported model (V,B,P)
            |     + dlt resolution and SNC preparation
            |     + supported MMP with Kähler targets
            |     + threefold contraction theorem
            |           + finite null locus contraction
            |           + conormal layers
            |           + prime-floor extension
            |     + discrepancy increase
            |     + special resolution
            |
            +-- Floor generation: A|_S semiample
            |     + strata adjunction
            |     + restriction criterion / links
            |     + finite isotropy images
            |     + norm products and gluing
            |
            +-- Supported lifting: A|_S semiample => kappa >= 1
                  + root covers and finite thickenings
                  + obstruction as graded derivation
                  + split residue insertion
                  + filtered Hodge modules
                  + symbol-kernel vanishing
                  + cyclic invariants and section growth
            |
            +-- Contradiction: kappa(V,A)=0 and kappa(V,A)>=1.
            |
            +-- Therefore M=0 and mD is trivial.

PART VI. GLOSSARY AND NOTATION
============================================================

31. Glossary
------------
Adjunction:
  The rule that restricts a canonical or adjoint line to a divisor and
  accounts for the normal bundle by adding a boundary term.

Analytically nef:
  A line bundle whose curvature is almost nonnegative in the analytic
  sense: bounded below by -epsilon times a Kähler form for every
  epsilon > 0.

Canonical class K_X:
  The line bundle of holomorphic top-degree forms.  Its first Chern
  class measures curvature.

Dlt:
  Divisorial log terminal.  A mild singularity condition allowing
  coefficient-one boundary components with simple normal crossings.

Effective:
  A divisor with nonnegative coefficients.

Floor:
  The reduced union of coefficient-one components of a boundary.

Iitaka dimension kappa:
  A measure of how many independent sections positive multiples of a
  line have and how large their images are.

Kähler:
  A complex manifold with a positive closed (1,1)-form.  It has a
  Riemannian metric compatible with the complex structure.

Klt:
  Kawamata log terminal.  A stronger mildness condition: exceptional
  crepant coefficients are strictly below 1.

Line bundle:
  A family of complex lines attached to points of a space.  Sections
  are holomorphic ways to choose a point in each line.

Nef:
  Intuitively, "not bending outward."  Curve-theoretically, nonnegative
  degree on every curve.  Analytically, almost nonnegative curvature.

Q-Cartier:
  Some positive integer multiple is Cartier, i.e. locally defined by
  one holomorphic equation.

Semiample:
  Some positive multiple is generated by global sections.

SNC:
  Simple normal crossing.  Local equations look like products of
  coordinate functions.

32. Notation
------------
X, V, Z:
  Complex spaces.  Usually compact Kähler, normal, connected.

Delta, B, Gamma:
  Effective rational Weil divisors, usually boundaries of pairs.

K_X:
  Canonical class.

A = K_X + Delta or K_V + B:
  The actual adjoint line.

S = floor(B):
  The reduced floor, the union of coefficient-one boundary components.

P:
  A nonzero effective rational Q-Cartier divisor representing the
  adjoint on a supported model.

L = O_V(qA):
  A sufficiently divisible actual Cartier multiple.

G = qP:
  The divisor of the chosen section of L.

E:
  The reduced Cartier divisor produced by the root cover.
