The packet construction has three stages: a finite set of lattices attached to each ideal class of OO_K, a real parameter in the unit torus, and a normalising scalar making covolumes equal to one. Wholly absorbed in the first stage is the order-index dependence, and it is exactly what makes the packet's arithmetic visible at the ramified primes.

## The scalar term

## The additive equidistribution proposition

Combining Lemma~lem:add-nonscalar with (eq:add-scalar)
yields the following distribution statement.

[Additive equidistribution]

Fix data m,Phi,chi,Ω_∞ as above. There are

every residue r prime to prod_{pin S}p and every integer pair

  int_{z_iequiv xi_1, z_jequiv xi_2!!pmod r}
  phi(z)prod_{pin S}f_p^*(z),dz
  =frac{A_phi l^{-2}}{r^2}prod_{pin S}d_p(m)
   +O_{m,Phi,chi,Ω_∞}(X^{-eps_F}),

Tr z=m.


Proof. 
By (eq:add-poisson) and Lemma~lem:add-nonscalar, the
integral of phiprod_{pin S}f_p^*(z) against any fixed character
psi_{r,xi}(z)=psi_{r}(xi_1z_i+xi_2z_j) of Z/rZ gets

xi_1=xi_2=0 (the scalar value of psi_{r,xi} on the trace

# Averaging the unramified factors

Proposition~prop:additive-equidistribution distributes the
nonnegative mass phi(z)prod_{pin S}f_p^*(z) uniformly among
residue classes at primes outside S. To prove
Theorem~thm:exterior-count, we must insert the remaining
factors b_p(z) while retaining their local means. Some of those means
are less than one, so the argument must also keep track of the
probability that a prime does not divide any component of z. We
first obtain a weighted sieve estimate with this feature, and then
combine its local means with the Euler factors in
Proposition~prop:ext-multiplicity-majorant.

## Residue functions and the local bounds

We recall from Lemma~lem:ext-unramified-data the following

    in R of  V_b with Q bounded}
]
with area(R_h)=O(h^4) up to fixed factors. Averaging
over the diagonal flow, the same argument that produced the ball
bound of Proposition~prop:balls now uses the exterior count of
Proposition~prop:weighted-exterior and gives
[
  mu{Q=m, ΛinE_b, |coordinates|<h}
  ll_{m,Phi,chi} h^4
]
for every sufficiently small h>0. Letting h↓0 along
h=1/n gives mu{Q=m, ΛinE_b}=0.


## Identification of the limit

Proof. [Completion of the proof of Theorem~thm:main]
Let (mu_i) be a sequence of packet measures with X_ito∞ and
let mu be any weak probability limit. Tightness
(Proposition~prop:tightness) reduces to the A_4-invariant
probability case. Write the A_4-ergodic decomposition
mu=intnu_z,dtau(z). Corollary~cor:positive-components
gives h_{nu_z}(a(1))gelambda_a/3 for tau-a.e. z. By
Theorem~thm:ekl and Proposition~prop:rig-obstruction, each
A_4-ergodic component is either invariant probability on an orbit
Λ J with J=SL_4(R), which is m_4, or else has
nu_z(E_b)=1 for some binB.

By Proposition~prop:null-exterior,
mu(E_b)=0 for each binB. Hence
tau-a.e. component is m_4, and therefore mu=m_4.

This identifies every subsequential limit as m_4. Tightness implies
the whole sequence converges weakly to m_4, which gives the
convergence statement in Theorem~thm:main. The same tightness
supplies, for every eps>0, a compact set carrying at least
1-eps of every sufficiently late packet probability, which is the
no-escape assertion.
